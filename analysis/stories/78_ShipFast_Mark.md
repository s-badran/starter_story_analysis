# Story Index: Mark Liu - ShipFast ($1.5M/Year Solopreneur, Ship 30+ Products Framework)

**Founder:** Mark Liu (30+ failed products, now solopreneur builder)
**Company:** ShipFast (SaaS ship tracking, $1.5M/year revenue)
**Model:** Build, launch, iterate, move to next
**Philosophy:** Shipping > perfection, volume > one big bet
**Speed:** Launch new product every 2-3 weeks
**Approach:** Learn from failures, compound knowledge

---

## 1. The Failure Journey - 30+ Products

**Mark's Background:**
- Not a CS major (overcome imposter syndrome)
- Started building products early
- First 20-30 products: Failed
- Learned from each failure

**The Failures (Key Learnings):**
- Product 1-5: "Nobody wanted it"
- Product 6-10: "Launched too early, had bugs"
- Product 11-15: "People liked it, but I didn't know how to grow it"
- Product 16-20: "Growth channel didn't work at scale"
- Product 21-30: "Better execution, but wrong market"

**Pattern Recognition:**
- Each failure = lesson applied to next
- Volume approach = faster learning
- 30 failures taught more than 1 big success
- Became comfortable with shipping imperfect

---

## 2. The ShipFast Origin Story

**The Problem:**
- Mark ordering things online constantly
- Shipping notifications from every retailer
- Scattered across emails, texts, SMS
- No unified place to track

**The Realization:**
- "I'm probably not alone in this"
- Bet: Others frustrated too
- Opportunity: Centralized shipping tracker

**Initial Validation:**
- Built MVP in 3 days
- Posted on Product Hunt
- Got traction immediately
- First users loved it

---

## 3. ShipFast - What It Does

**Product:**
- Unified shipping tracker
- Connect Amazon, Shopify, eBay, etc.
- All orders in one dashboard
- Real-time tracking updates
- Mobile app (iOS + Android)

**Core Value:**
- No more hunting for tracking numbers
- Single source of truth
- Saves time
- Peace of mind on package status

**Users:**
- Consumers (people who order a lot)
- Ecommerce business owners
- Anyone with multiple shopping accounts

**Revenue Model:**
- Free tier (limited tracking)
- Premium: $9.99/month (unlimited)
- Business tier: $25/month (team features)

## 7. The Solopreneur Model

**Why Mark Stays Solo:**

**Advantage 1: Speed**
- All decisions = instant
- No meetings, no consensus
- Ship 3x faster than teams

**Advantage 2: Margins**
- No team overhead
- $1.5M revenue, maybe $1.2M profit
- Scales without hiring
- Bootstrap friendly

**Advantage 3: Focus**
- Know codebase completely
- Know user base completely
- Focused on what matters
- Not managing people

**Advantage 4: Freedom**
- Work when wants to
- Build what wants to
- Keep 100% ownership
- No VC pressure

**The Trade-off:**
- Can't do everything
- Customer support = his time
- Limited to what one person can build
- Hits ceiling eventually

## 8. The Operating Model

**Weekly Rhythm:**
- Monday: Review customer feedback
- Tuesday-Wednesday: Build features/fixes
- Thursday: Test + polish
- Friday: Ship + monitor
- Weekends: Off or light work

**Customer Support:**
- Mark handles personally
- Twitter DMs + email
- Fast responses = competitive advantage
- People love talking to founder

**Feature Development:**
- Build what users ask for (90%)
- Build Mark's ideas (10%)
- Prioritize by frequency of requests
- Ship incrementally, not big releases

**Metrics Tracked:**
- DAU (daily active users)
- Free to premium conversion
- Churn rate (target: <3% monthly)
- Feature requests (what to build next)
- Support response time (keep <1 hour)

- Learned growth channels
- Learned what works / doesn't
- ShipFast = best practices applied

**Learning 5: Copy Winners**

- Pricing from Notion, Figma

- UI inspiration from iOS ecosystem

## 11. The Pitfalls Avoided

**Pitfall 1: Hiring Too Early**
- Many founders: "Need team to scale"
- Mark: Solo until 800K users
- Better economics, faster decisions
- Hired only when can't do more alone

**Pitfall 2: Perfectionism**
- First 20 products: Perfectionist
- Waited 3 months to launch
- Users left before feedback
- ShipFast: Launched with bugs
- Iterated based on real usage

**Pitfall 3: Wrong Market**
- 30 products: Some were great ideas
- But wrong market (too niche)
- ShipFast: Large market (everyone buys online)
- Timing: Right when ecommerce booming

**Pitfall 4: Premature Scaling**
- Could raise VC
- Would pressure to hire, expand
- Would destroy solopreneur model
- Chose profitability over growth-at-all-costs

---

## 12. Advice from Mark

**Piece 1: Ship Imperfect, Iterate Perfect**
- Launch with bugs
- Get real users
- Fix based on usage
- Faster than waiting for perfection

**Piece 2: Copy Winning Models**
- Don't need to invent everything
- Study successful apps
- Copy what works (pricing, UX, model)
- Apply to your market

**Piece 3: Founder-Market Fit Matters**
- Use your own product
- Solve problems you have
- Passion carries through
- Easier to persist through hard times

**Piece 4: Volume Beats Single Bets**
- Ship 30 products > bet everything on 1
- Learn faster
- More chances to hit
- Compounding knowledge

**Piece 5: Solopreneur Possible (For Certain Products)**
- Don't assume need team
- Stay solo while possible
- Better economics
- Faster decisions
