## 10. The Strategic Decisions

**Decision 1: Platform Strategy Over Original**
- Could build standalone form builder
- Could compete with Typeform, Jotform
- Chose: Build on Notion instead
- Reasoning: Larger market (Notion users), less competition, clearer niche

**Decision 2: Freemium Over Paid-Only**
- Could launch paid-only
- Chose: Freemium model
- Reasoning: Lower friction, viral feedback loop, conversion natural

**Decision 3: Stay in Lane**
- Could expand to CRM, database, etc.
- Chose: Focus on forms only
- Reasoning: Dominate niche first, expand later

**Decision 4: Bootstrap Over VC**
- Could raise VC
- Chose: Bootstrap
- Reasoning: Profitable from early, keep autonomy, slower growth is fine

**Decision 5: Community First Over Sales**
- Could hire sales team
- Chose: Community + organic
- Reasoning: Product-market fit obvious, no sales needed, community sustainable

- Notion's forms bad = NotForms good

**Lesson 2: Community > Customers**
- Build for makers/indie community

**Lesson 3: Stay in Lane**
- Do one thing better than anyone

**Lesson 4: Freemium = Viral**
- Free tier = free trial for everyone

**Lesson 5: Organic = Long-term**
