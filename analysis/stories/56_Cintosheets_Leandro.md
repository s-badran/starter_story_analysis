# Story Index: Leandro - Cintosheets ($9K/month, 4 years)

**Founder:** Leandro (Buenos Aires, Argentina)
**Company:** Cintosheets (Notion → Google Sheets sync plugin)
**Revenue:** $9K/month ($108K annual), 4+ years
**Users:** 70K+ users, 450+ paying customers
**Lifetime Revenue:** ~$120K+ over 4 years
**MVP Timeline:** Built in 2 weeks
**Platform:** Google Sheets/Notion add-on

---

## 1. The Idea - Solving a Real Gap

**Problem:**
Notion and Google Sheets integration didn't exist (or was poor). Users wanted to sync Notion data to Sheets.

**How He Found It:**
- 4 years ago, building integration between Sheets and another service
- Gained knowledge of Sheets API
- Notion released official API
- Realized: "There was a gap between what users needed and what's available in Notion"

**Validation Method:**
- Went to Reddit
- Searched r/Notion for keywords: "Sheets," "Google Sheets," "Excel," "CSV"
- Found many people trying to export Notion data to Sheets
- Validated: Real people, real need, no solution

## 5. The Platform Opportunity - Zapier Model

**Why Zapier/Notion Marketplace Works:**

1. **Built-in audience** - People already in platform looking for integrations
2. **Trust transfer** - If approved by platform, deemed trustworthy
3. **Conversion optimization** - People actively searching for this solution
4. **Low competition** - Not everyone can build on platforms

**Cintosheets Example:**
- Listed in Notion marketplace
- People searching for "Notion Sheets sync" → find Cintosheets
- Marketplace approval = credibility

---

## 6. Finding More Opportunities - The Framework

**Leandro's Discovery Process for Others:**

"I believe Zapier is a good place to go and find those kind of opportunities."

**The Method:**
1. Go to Zapier
2. Check most common "zap" pairs (integrations people want)
3. Find gaps: integrations that are "messy" or broken
4. Example: QuickBooks → Sheets is painful in Zapier
5. Build native integration for that specific pair

**Why It Works:**
- Clear market demand (proven by Zapier usage data)
- Pain point visible (Zapier workarounds are clunky)
- TAM known (number of Zapier users doing this integration)
- Easy to validate quickly

---

Notion-Sheets integration isn't sexy. But it's profitable and sticky.

## Summary

Leandro built a $9K/month platform add-on by:

1. **Identifying platform gap** - Notion API + Sheets API = missing integration
2. **Validating on Reddit** - Real users, real need, keyword evidence
3. **Building MVP fast** - 2 weeks, not months
4. **Launching in marketplace** - Built-in distribution + approval credibility
5. **Maintaining steady growth** - 70K users, 450 paying, $9K/month over 4 years

Most importantly: Leandro proves that boring, utility-focused add-ons can provide sustainable income for indie hackers. Not every business needs to be a venture-backed unicorn. Sometimes $9K/month for 4+ years beats nothing at all.
