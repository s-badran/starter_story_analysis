## 3. The Five-Step Framework - Build → Sell

**Step 1: Set Goals**
- Define exit timeline (3, 6, 12 months?)
- This determines product roadmap, development speed, and scaling needs

**Step 2: Validation**
- Use landing page builder (Webflow, Framer, etc.)
- Distribute via X/Twitter (build in public)
- Target: 15%+ conversion rate on landing page = good signal
- Goal: Get emails from interested early users

**Step 3: Build with Feedback Loop**
- Build ONE key feature first
- Send product to email list subscribers
- Gather feedback → improve → relaunch
- Iterate: build → feedback → edit → rebuild

**Step 4: Growth**
- Key strategy: Build in Public (Twitter, Reddit, Product Hunt)
- Organic growth is priority (no paid ads for micro-apps)
- Show traction and momentum to potential buyers

**Step 5: Package & Sell**
- Make business an asset others can run
- Key metrics buyers care about:
  - ARR (annual recurring revenue)
  - Revenue growth rate
  - Gross margins
  - Average revenue per user (ARPU)
  - Lifetime value (LTV)
  - Customer acquisition cost (CAC)
  - Churn rate
- Goal: Optimize these before selling

## 4. The Difference Maker - Timing & Trends

**Key Insight:**
All successful products started from trends and sentiment analysis on Twitter/X.

**Example:**
- When AI was hot, AI tools sold
- Products that rode trends: higher success rate
- Products built in off-trend moments: struggle

**Timing Matters:**
"The main difference between the projects is based on the trend you were running and the moment. So basically the timing, that's the main difference."

## 8. Key Lessons for Builders

**Lesson 1: Timing Beats Everything**
- Same product, different time = different outcome
- Follow trends, but don't chase dying trends

**Lesson 2: Persist Through Rejection**
- Being rejected for job opportunity (2024) became blessing

**Lesson 3: Execution Speed Matters**
- 30-day cycles force prioritization
- MVP over perfection
- Ship fast, iterate based on market

**Lesson 4: Build for Acquisition**
- Design business to be sellable
- Keep operations clean
- Document systems
- Optimize metrics buyers care about

**Lesson 5: Know Your Why**
- Domenico's why: escape 9-to-5, build something bigger

## 10. The Build & Sell vs. Scale & Raise Trade-off

**Build & Sell Advantages:**
- Certainty: exit with real money in days/months
- Resilience: failure only costs 1-2 months per project
- Learning: 7 products = 7× the learning iterations
- Freedom: no VC pressure

**vs. Scale & Raise:**
- VC model: 10 years, maybe $0 or $100M
- Build & Sell: 3-7 years, guaranteed revenue from exits

**Domenico's Choice:**
Mix of both: sold 6 micro-products for speed/learning, then scaled Softgen with revenue.

## Summary

Domenico proved that:

1. **Speed compounds learning** - 7 products in 3 years = vastly more pattern recognition than most founders get in 10 years

2. **Trends are real** - Most successful products rode AI wave, making 2024 and beyond prime time for trend-riding

3. **Build-to-exit is viable** - $1M in 3 years from selling is better than many VC-backed paths

4. **Framework beats luck** - The 5-step system (goal → validate → build → grow → sell) is replicable

5. **Timing beats execution** - Same product in Feb vs August can 10x in success

**For aspiring builders:** Domenico shows you don't need to pick one idea and bet 10 years on it. You can build 7 things, learn from each, and then go all-in on the one that clicks.
