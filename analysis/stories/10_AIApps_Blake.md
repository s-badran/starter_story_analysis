## 5. Pricing

**Model:** Subscription

**Strategy:** Lower price points for accessibility
- More users = more word-of-mouth
- Better user sentiment
- More sustainable growth
- Use SuperWall for A/B testing

**Pricing Tactics:**
- Test different price points
- Test different placements (paywall positions in app)
- Split test weekly vs. yearly
- Iterate constantly

**Result:** Optimal pricing can 1.5-2x revenue per user

## 10. Challenges & Lessons

**Key Lesson: Create Urgency**
- Most productive when high risk
- Riz GPT: Parents' house on market, broke, friends successful
- Umax: On brother's couch, spent Thanksgiving coding alone
- Apex: Everyone doubting move from apps to content
- "Feel the pressure and it fires me up"

**Lessons on Marketing:**
- Influencer outreach is hard (don't give up after 10 DMs)
- Required persistence (DMing moms, joining Discords)
- Small payments to underground creators work best
- Expect many rejections

**App Development Learnings:**
- Bad apps can still succeed if marketing is good
- Notifications, reviews, design polish secondary to distribution
- Paywall placement matters (hard paywall converts)
- SuperWall A/B testing essential

**Advice to Entrepreneurs:**
1. **Abstract from social media noise**
   - Don't just copy what others say to do
   - Think from first principles
   - "Naval quote: If you want wrong decision, ask everybody"

2. **Stop overthinking, start doing**
   - Don't know what to build? Build something
   - Don't know what to tweet? Tweet anything
   - Learn by doing, iterate fast

3. **Create urgency**
   - High risk = high productivity
   - Find what fires you up
   - Use pressure as fuel

4. **Consistency matters**
   - 5-10 minutes daily beats perfect occasional effort
   - Small actions compound
   - Momentum beats perfection
