**Major Challenge:** Inventory management

**Key Learnings:**
1. **Build a brand, not just product** (long-term value)
2. **Inventory planning is critical** in physical products
3. **UGC is king** (user-generated content outperforms ads)
4. **Positioning matters** (luxury vs. dropship)
5. **Know when to build vs. buy** (manufacturing partnerships)

**Advice:**
- "Make some shit happen"
- Don't wait for perfect conditions
- Compound success (small wins → big wins)
- Play long-term game (brand building)
- "Moral obligation to copy if something works" → make it better
