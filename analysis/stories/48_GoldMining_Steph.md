## 1. The Challenge

**Starting Point:**
Steph wanted to prove that AI could be used systematically to find business ideas—not randomly asking ChatGPT "what should I build?" but following a repeatable, data-driven process.

**Goal:**
Create a structured framework that anyone could use in 45 minutes to go from zero market knowledge to a validated business idea with a landing page.

---

## 2. The Framework Overview

**Name:** Gold Mining Framework
**Time Investment:** 45 minutes total
**Components:**
- 6 specific tools
- 5 strategic prompts
- Clear sequential process

**Philosophy:**
Market data exists everywhere. The bottleneck isn't data availability—it's knowing where to look and how to synthesize insights into actionable business ideas.

## 3. Gold Mining Process - Step by Step

### **Step 1: Market Expansion (5 minutes)**

**Objective:** Identify a broad market with proven demand and growth signals.

**Method:**
- Use Claude to analyze industry reports
- Look for markets with growing consumer spending
- Identify emerging pain points
- Filter for markets with $500M+ TAM minimum

**Example:** Co-parenting (custody, blended families, divorce management)
- Growing market: 40% of US kids live in multi-household situations
- Growing pain: Modern technology hasn't caught up to modern family structures
- TAM: Billions in potential services

---

### **Step 2: Demand Validation (5 minutes)**

**Objective:** Confirm that target customers actively seek solutions.

**Method:**
- Search subreddits for the market
- Look for high-engagement posts (100+ comments)
- Identify specific problems being discussed
- Quantify: How many discussions? How many people?

**Example - r/Parenting & r/CoDeparenting:**
- Post: "How do you keep kids' schedules aligned between houses?"
- Engagement: 200+ comments
- Insight: Parents desperately want a solution but haven't found one

**Key Signal:**
If people are discussing it on Reddit, demand is proven. No need for expensive market research.

---

### **Step 3: Reddit Mining (10-15 minutes)**

**Objective:** Extract specific problems and solutions from real user conversations.

**Method:**
- Manually read top Reddit posts in the market
- Identify recurring pain points
- Document specific quotes from users
- Note suggested solutions (often users describe workarounds)

**Example Problems Found:**
- "Syncing basketball practice times between mom's and dad's house"
- "Remembering what clothes are at which house"
- "Coordinating pickup/dropoff without texting multiple people"
- "Keeping track of birthdays/holidays across households"

**Example Solution Indicators:**
- Users mention Google Sheets (manual workaround)
- Users suggest apps don't exist yet
- Users mention paying for solutions currently (expensive or imperfect)

---

### **Step 4: AI Synthesis (5 minutes)**

**Objective:** Turn raw Reddit data into prioritized, ranked business ideas.

**Method:**
- Feed Reddit insights to Claude
- Provide 5 structured prompts:
  1. "What are the top 5 specific problems?"
  2. "What's the #1 most urgent problem?"
  3. "What's the monetization opportunity?"
  4. "Who is the target customer?"
  5. "What are 3 possible product angles?"

**Example - Prompt Output:**
- #1 Problem: Schedule synchronization across households
- Customer Segment: Co-parenting couples (40% of families)
- Opportunity: Recurring subscription ($10-15/month minimum)
- Positioning: "Google Calendar for co-parents"

---

### **Step 5: Landing Page Generation (5-10 minutes)**

**Objective:** Create a professional landing page to test if the idea resonates.

**Tools:** Lovable (no-code app builder + Claude integration)

**Process:**
- Claude generates landing page copy
- Lovable generates visual design
- Result: Professional landing page in minutes

**Landing Page Components:**
- Hero section: Clear value proposition
- Problem section: "85% of co-parents struggle with schedule sync"
- Solution section: How the app works
- Pricing section: Tiered subscription ($9/month basic, $19/month premium)
- Testimonial section: Quotes from Reddit users
- CTA: Email signup for beta access

---

## 6. The Five Strategic Prompts

**Prompt 1: Problem Extraction**
"From this Reddit thread data, what are the 5 most frequently mentioned problems? Rank by frequency."

**Prompt 2: Problem Prioritization**
"Of these problems, which one would customers pay the most to solve? Why?"

**Prompt 3: Monetization**
"What's the pricing power? How would you structure pricing to maximize revenue?"

**Prompt 4: Customer Definition**
"Who is the exact target customer? Age, income, mindset, technology adoption?"

**Prompt 5: Product Angles**
"What are 3 completely different angles to solve this problem? Which is most defensible?"

## 9. Framework Philosophy

**Why This Works:**
1. **Leverages existing data** - Reddit is a goldmine of customer problems
2. **Removes bias** - Data-driven, not founder instinct
3. **Validates demand first** - If not discussed on Reddit, problem may not exist
4. **Fast iteration** - 45 minutes to test, then pivot if needed
5. **Scalable** - Same process works for any market

**The Business Implication:**
This is the future of startup ideation. Rather than:
- Wasting months on perfect products nobody wants
- Pitching ideas based on intuition
- Building before validating

You can:
- Validate demand in 45 minutes
- Test 10 ideas in one day
- Find underserved markets systematically
