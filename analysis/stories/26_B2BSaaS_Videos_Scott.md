## 15. Lessons & Insights for First-Time Builders

**Green Flags (What Works):**
- Choose specific niche (B2B SaaS videos)
- Build audience consistently (1 hour/week on Twitter)
- Productize service (standardized packages)
- Price for value, not hourly
- Hire team to scale beyond founder time
- Use niching to raise prices 10x
- Multiple channels (Twitter + DMs + cold email)

**Red Flags (What Doesn't Work):**
- Generalist video work (too competitive)
- Hourly pricing (limits scale)
- No audience building (relies on cold outreach only)
- Solo delivery (bottleneck)

**Actionable Frameworks:**
1. **Niching:** Choose specific industry (B2B SaaS)
2. **Content Strategy:** 1 hour/week on Twitter (educational)
3. **Productization:** Create 3 tiers of packages
4. **Pricing:** 10x from initial; price for expertise
5. **Outreach:** Twitter + LinkedIn DMs + cold email
6. **Scaling:** Hire contractors to fulfill

**Advice to Young Founders:**
- "Build specific skill first (video in Scott's case)"
- "Monetize at higher level (creative → strategic)"
- "Choose niche (specialization = premium pricing)"
- "Post consistently (audience = distribution)"
- "Price for value, not time"
- "Hire team early (scale beyond yourself)"
