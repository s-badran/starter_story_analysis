## 3. The YouTube Strategy - The Game Changer

**Why YouTube:**
- Best trust-building platform
- "People buy from people they know, like, and trust"
- Longer form = deep relationship building
- Beats Twitter, TikTok, blogging for B2B SaaS

**Vasco's Framework:**

**Component 1: Money-Making Assets**
- Videos that generate sales directly
- Three content buckets:

**Bucket 1: Evergreen**
- Videos staying relevant for years
- Example: "Best AI SEO Writers"
- Gets views 1-2+ years after upload
- 65% from YouTube search, 20% from Google

**Bucket 2: News/Trends**
- Timely content (gets views first 2-3 days)
- "Google Update dropped" style
- Gets visibility spike, builds audience
- Funnels new viewers to whole channel

**Bucket 3: Viral/Entertainment**
- MrBeast-style production applied to software
- Hardest to replicate
- But when works, massive reach

**The Flywheel:**
- Post consistently → Get views → Viewers become subscribers → Subscribers become customers

---

## 4. The YouTube Playbook - Three Core Steps

**Step 1: Produce Money-Making Assets**
- Not just entertainment
- Each video has potential to sell
- Mix evergreen + news + viral
- Consistency matters

**Step 2: Multiply Yourself**
- Can't make unlimited videos solo
- Created multiple YouTube channels:
  - VascoSEO (main)
  - SEO10 (general SEO)
  - TimSEO (trending)
  - Etc.
- Hired creators (found on Upwork, people comfortable on camera)
- Paid per-performance (not per-hour)
- Expanded to different languages (Portuguese, Spanish, etc.)

**Step 3: Scale with Paid Ads**
- Organic videos get views then plateau
- Use paid YouTube "in-feed ads"
- Retest videos that worked organically
- Showed in YouTube homepage + sidebar
- Looked like organic content
- Drove additional views when organic died

## 5. Tracking YouTube Performance - The Data Stack

**Challenge:** Hard to track YouTube organic traffic

**Vasco's Solution:**

**Method 1: Dedicated UTM Parameters**
- Each video gets unique UTM code
- Links in descriptions
- ~1% click-through (but confirms source)

**Method 2: Unique Coupon Codes**
- "Go to arvo.com and click chat, ask for code from [Video Name]"
- Support team notes which video brought customer
- Tracks which videos convert to sales

**Method 3: Welcome Questionnaire**
- When users sign up, asked "Where did you hear?"
- Choose from list of YouTube creators/channels
- Direct attribution

**Result:** Clear understanding of:
- Which videos drive sales
- Which creators perform best
- Which channels generate ROI

## 6. The CTA Strategy - Moving Actions Forward

**Original Approach:**
- CTA at end of video
- Problem: Most people don't watch to end

**The Fix:**
- CTA at beginning AND middle
- Interactive CTAs (not just "check out website")
- Specific actions ("Click link to get coupon")

**Impact:**
- Moved CTAs = higher signups
- Interactive CTAs > generic ones
- Result: Significant increase in conversions

## Summary

Vasco demonstrates:

1. **YouTube as Primary Channel**
   - Best for B2B SaaS trust-building
   - Long-form content = relationship
   - Face/voice = connection

2. **Content Bucket Strategy**
   - Evergreen (long-lasting)
   - News/trends (quick spike)
   - Viral/entertainment (highest reach)
   - Mix keeps audience engaged

3. **Multiplication Matters**
   - Solo limited to few videos/week
   - Multi-channel approach: Hire creators
   - Same playbook, different channels
   - Different languages = new markets

4. **Paid Ads Leverage Organic**
   - Don't start with paid
   - Start with organic to find winners
   - Then pay to re-amplify winners
   - More efficient spending

5. **Tracking is Critical**
   - UTM codes
   - Coupon codes
   - Questionnaires
   - Understand which content sells

6. **Consistency Over Perfection**
   - 45 days of daily videos beats 1 perfect video
   - Compounding effect
   - Volume > Polish

7. **Simple Tools Win**
   - Loom + Miro + Camtasia
   - Good hardware > fancy software
   - Accessibility matters (anyone can do this)

**For SaaS Founders:** Vasco proves YouTube as sole growth channel can sustain $70K/month revenue. No paid ads needed initially. The cost is consistency and willingness to be on camera.
