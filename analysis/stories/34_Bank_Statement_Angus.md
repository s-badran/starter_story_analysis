## 1. Problem & Validation

**Problem Identified:** Bank statements come as PDFs; impossible to analyze in Excel

**Personal Background:**
- Quit job (wanted to build own business)
- Planned to build something else initially
- Needed analysis of bank data for personal finances
- Discovered manual bank statement conversion tedious
- Thought: "If this is hard for me, must be hard for others"

**Validation Method:**
- Built local version first (Kotlin console app)
- Tested with friends' bank statements
- Generalized solution across multiple bank formats
- Built MVP with friend in Next.js + Netlify + AWS
- Bought Google Ads to test demand
- People immediately uploaded statements → validated problem

## 4. Go-to-Market & Customer Acquisition

**PRIMARY CHANNEL: Google Search Ads → Organic Search**

**Strategy:**
- Phase 1: Google Ads ($1K spend → $300 returns) - never profitable
- Phase 2: Realized organic users more valuable than paid
- Phase 3: Quit ads, focused on organic discovery + product improvement
- Result: 2-3 organic signups daily became stable

**Secondary Channels:**
- **Blogging** - Attention but no conversions
- **Building in Public** - Followers, no real users
- **Cold Email** - 1 sale in 3 months, annoyed people
- **Organic Discovery** - Became primary (Google SEO)

**Lesson:** Kill what doesn't work, double down on organic

## 15. Lessons & Insights for First-Time Builders

**Green Flags (What Works):**
- Solve own problem first (validation)
- MVP in one week (not months)
- Kill paid ads if unprofitable early
- Organic SEO (long-term play)
- Simple product (one feature)
- Solopreneur model (high margins)
- Patience (2-3 years runway saved)
- Listen to users (improve based on feedback)

**Red Flags (What Doesn't Work):**
- Paid ads (test small, kill fast if not working)
- Social media presence (vanity)
- Blogging without conversions
- Cold email (annoying)
- Multiple features (kills clarity)
- No runway saved (pressure kills thinking)

**Actionable Frameworks:**
1. **MVP Speed:** 1 week max
2. **Distribution Test:** Spend $1K on 3 channels, pick winner
3. **Organic Focus:** No paid ads if CAC > LTV
4. **Runway:** Save 2-3 years (patience compounds)
5. **Product:** Simple > feature-rich
6. **Solopreneur:** No employees (higher margins)

**Advice to Young Angus:**
- "Save money first (runway buys patience)"
- "One feature > complex product"
- "Real users > fake validation"
- "Solopreneur margin > growth rate"
- "Ignore what others say works; test empirically"
