# Story Index: Accessibility SaaS - Ryan Chen / Neuro Gum

**Video ID:** BeRKQkU5Ceo
**Founders:** Ryan Chen (paralyzed at 19) + Kent (college best friend)
**Company:** Neuro (caffeinated gum for focus/energy)
**Status:** $10M/month in revenue, $100M+ company valuation
**Location:** USA
**Key Achievement:** Built $100M company from $0, overcame paralysis + bankruptcy threats, leveraged Shark Tank strategically

---

## 1. Problem & Validation

**Personal Problem:**
- Ryan & Kent training for Olympic/Paralympic teams
- Taking pills during training (energy, focus, lucid dreaming formulas)
- Pills were uncomfortable to take, formulas from Kent's dorm room

**Aha Moment:**
- On scuba diving trip (Groupon deal), met people
- Realized: Wouldn't want to share pills with strangers
- "What if we put this in gum instead of pills?"

**Validation Method:**
- Personal need (they used it themselves)
- Saw market pain (energy supplements uncomfortable to consume)
- Tested with friends/coworkers at Hulu/Sony Music

---

## 2. Industry & Market Opportunities

**Market:** Functional beverages/supplements (multibillion dollar industry)

**Positioning:** "Accessibility through format" - same benefits as pills/coffee, but gum form

**Problem Size:** Millions want energy/focus without awkwardness of pills

**Timing:** Nootropics becoming mainstream; functional gums emerging

---

## 3. Product & MVP Strategy

**Neuro Core:**
- Gum format (vs. pills or drinks)
- Caffeinated + focus ingredients (Racetam, Paracetam, creatine)
- Tested multiple formulas in lab

**MVP Development:**
- R&D phase: 1 year of finding manufacturer willing to work with them
- Samples: Tested with co-workers at Hulu/Sony
- Feedback: "I was up till 3am, couldn't sleep" = product works

**Challenge:**
- No manufacturer wanted to work with unknown team
- Had to find specialist willing to take risk

## 4. Go-to-Market & Customer Acquisition

## 15. Lessons & Insights for First-Time Builders

**Green Flags (What Works):**
- Solve own problem (you understand deeply)
- Founder story matters (paralysis = compelling narrative)
- Crowdfunding validates demand + raises capital
- Earned media > paid media (authentic endorsement)
- Celebrity use (organic, not paid) = credibility
- Premium positioning (gum perceived as premium)
- Team culture (96% satisfaction = lower churn)

**Red Flags (What Doesn't Work):**
- Manufacturing complexity (1 year to find partner)
- Supply chain fragility (border delays, freight costs)
- Legal vulnerability (trademark lawsuit nearly bankrupted)
- No diversified team at start (just 2 founders)

**Actionable Frameworks:**
1. **Problem Identification:** Find personal pain you actively experience
2. **Crowdfunding MVP:** Use Indiegogo/Kickstarter to validate + raise capital
3. **Founder Story:** Authentic narrative (paralysis, journey) = marketing asset
4. **Product Format:** Innovation in format (gum) = differentiation
5. **Strategic Advisors:** Get mentors who have won before (Lubetysky example)

**Advice to Young Ryan:**
- "Bounce back higher from rock bottom"
- "Go to battle with best friend (trust > skill)"
- "Build culture first (96% satisfaction pays dividends)"
- "Earned media > paid (authenticity matters)"
