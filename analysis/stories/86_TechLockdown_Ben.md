# Story Index: Ben - TechLockdown ($15K/Month MRR, Content-Led Side Project)

**Founder:** Ben Bozet (Full-time developer turned solopreneur)
**Company:** TechLockdown (Internet accountability app)
**Achievement:** $15K+ MRR solopreneur, 2M+ organic visitors
**Model:** Consumer app + content marketing
**Approach:** Side project while employed, content-first GTM
**Status:** Fully solopreneur, zero VC, profitable from month 1

---

## 1. The Background - Serial Side Project Builder

**Career:**
- Software developer (day job)
- College: Side projects constantly
- Full-time job: Couldn't shut off builder brain

**Failed Side Projects:**
1. Lead generation for freelancers: Got obsessed with colors, logo, brand (no business model)
2. Landing page builder: Overcomplication, burnout before launch

- Perfectionism killed projects (1, 2)
- Teams complicated projects (3)

## 2. TechLockdown - The Origin

**The Problem:**
- 2020: Work from home normalized
- Everyone: Too much internet time
- Ben: Researched guardrails for own internet use
- Realization: "Really hard to do"

**The Solution:**
- Spent months building personal system
- Distilled into YouTube video

## 6. The GTM Strategy - Content Marketing

**The Approach:**
- Well-researched, helpful content (free)
- Make it shareable
- "Give it away for free"

**Example: "iPhone Dumbphone Guide"**
- How to convert iPhone into dumbphone
- Detailed step-by-step (confusing for most)
- One of best-performing guides
- Read hundreds of thousands of times

**Reddit Strategy:**
- Subtle promotion (not overt)
- Put value in Reddit post itself
- Mention YouTube/guide at end
- Can't be too promotional (Reddit rules)
- More engagement = higher ranking
- People explore further = find TechLockdown

**Key Posts:**
- "How to build Resume"
- "Top 10 questions during job hunt"
- "How to interview"
- "How to negotiate salary"
- Each = SEO hack using Reddit authority
- Link to product (subtle)

**Why Works:**
- Reddit has SEO power
- Don't ask for visit, provide value
- Trust builds through helpfulness
- Natural link to product

## 11. The Advice

**Piece 1: Validate Before Quitting**
- "Have a way to get customers"
- Must solve customer acquisition before leaving job
- Have plan you can scale up
- Not hope, proven approach

**Piece 2: Side Project, Not Job Escape**
- Don't start because hate job
- Start because genuinely passionate
- Passion carries through hard times
- Sustainable energy source

**Piece 3: Build Audience Before Product**
- Don't wait for perfect product
- Build audience on topic
- Launch to ready-made market
- Reduces risk

**Piece 4: Pick One Channel**
- Don't juggle 5 channels while employed
- Pick one you enjoy
- Do it really well
- Master one beats amateur five

**Piece 5: Content Builds Trust**
- Long game (6+ months)
- Free value first
- Monetize second
- Trust = customers

**Piece 6: Simple System Wins**
- Overcomplicated systems fail
- Simple system that works > perfect system that doesn't
- Ship simple, iterate after

## Summary

Ben demonstrates:

1. **Audience Before Product**
   - Build audience first
   - Launch ready-made market
   - Reduces customer acquisition risk

2. **Organic Growth + Content**
   - Free, helpful content
   - Subtle promotion (not overt)
   - Long-term trust building
   - Reddit authority leverage

3. **Solopreneur > Teams**
   - For this type: Solo more effective
   - Faster decisions
   - Higher margins
   - Authentic voice

4. **Time Management**
   - Early morning: High focus work
   - Evening: Lower focus marketing
   - Pick one channel (master it)
   - All doable while employed

5. **Validate Before Quitting**
   - Must have revenue
   - Must have proven acquisition channel
   - Safety net important
   - Then go all-in

6. **Passion > Escape**
   - Start because love problem
   - Not because hate job
   - Energy carries through
   - Authenticity obvious

**For Side Project Builders:** Ben's model (build audience + content + organic growth + solopreneur) proves viable for $15K+/month. Validate with audience before building product. Pick one channel and master it. Stay solo until forced to hire.
