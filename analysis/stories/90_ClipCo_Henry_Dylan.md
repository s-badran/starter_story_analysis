# Story Index: Henry & Dylan - ClipCo ($1.8M/Year, Content Studio at Scale)

**Founders:** Henry & Dylan (Brothers, content creators, animator minds)
**Company:** ClipCo (AI-powered video clip creation from long-form content)
**Achievement:** $1.8M annual revenue, 100M+ clips created, 1B+ total views
**Model:** B2B SaaS + content creation services
**Key Insight:** Creator economy + automation + taste = defensible business

---

## 2. The ClipCo Insight - AI Meets Taste

**The Observation:**
- Tools exist for clip creation
- Automated tools: All create bad clips
- Problem: No taste (missing context)
- Opportunity: Humans + AI for good clips

**The Solution:**
- Human editors (have taste)
- AI tools (have speed)
- Combined: Best of both
- Result: High-quality clips, fast creation

**Why Different:**
- Pure AI: No taste (bad clips)
- Pure manual: Too slow
- ClipCo: Hybrid = quality + speed

**Target Users:**

## 3. The Business Model - Hybrid Play

**Service Model (Primary Revenue):**
- Clients send long-form content
- ClipCo team edits into clips
- Deliver 20-50 clips per video
- Price: $1,000-5,000 per video (estimated)

**Software Model (Secondary):**
- AI tools for self-service clip creation
- Lower price ($99-500/month)
- For clients wanting DIY
- Less revenue but higher margin

**Combined Model:**
- Service: High revenue, lower margin
- Software: Lower revenue, high margin
- Both: Diversified, better economics
