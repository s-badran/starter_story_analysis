# Story Index: Chris - Wishlist App ($150K/year Side Project, Zero Marketing)

**Founder:** Chris (Full-stack developer, patience master)
**Company:** Wishlist (Wish list app for sharing with family)
**Revenue:** $150K/year ($12.5K/month), just quit day job
**Users:** 1.1M+ registered, 4K+ paying customers, 110K MAU
**Model:** Freemium + Premium
**Timeline:** 6 years (3 uni + 2.5 job), just went full-time
**Background:** Rejected by dream company, used rejection as fuel
**Strategy:** Small side project, user-driven development, 99%+ margins

---

## 1. The Origin - Rejection as Motivation

**Starting Point:**
- University student, wanted to work for cool bike brand
- Applied for app developer job (without experience)
- Got rejected BUT: Company said "We like your enthusiasm"
- Decision: "I'll learn to build apps, get into these companies"

**The Seed:** App development as career path unlocked

---

## 2. Finding the Idea - Solving Own Problem

**The Gap:**
- Obvious first app: To-do list (2M+ already exist)
- Too crowded
- Alternative: Wishlist app (solve own problem)
- Chris still using Excel spreadsheet for personal wishlist
- "Why is there no nice wishlist app?"

**The Insight:**
- Nicer version of notes app
- Store wishes for Christmas, birthdays
- Share with family/friends
- Simple but valuable

**Timeline:**
- Started: 2019 in university
- Released v1: 2020
- Launched v2: Late 2023 (major relaunch)

---

## 3. The Parallel Journey - Full-Time Job + Side Project

**The Setup:**
- 3 years: University + building Wishlist
- 2.5 years: Full-time dev job + Wishlist side project
- Total: 6 years to reach $150K/year

**Daily Routine (While Employed):**
- 8 AM: Wake up, 1 hour on Wishlist (emails, analytics, support)
- 9 AM - 6 PM: Full-time job (software development, paid to code)
- 6 PM - 8 PM: Personal time (sports, socializing)
- 8 PM - 11 PM+: Wishlist development

**Seasonal Variation:**
- Winter: Coded more (no outdoor activities)
- Summer: Harder to motivate coding after work
- Solution: Negotiated 4-day work week (2025) for extra Wishlist day

**Key Insight:** "Marathon, not sprint"

---

## 4. Learnings from 6-Year Journey

**Learning 1: Be Honest with Employer**
- Told boss about Wishlist
- Mutually beneficial: Learn at job, build on side
- Creates trust

**Learning 2: Use Every Free Minute**
- Car rides, train rides: Work on side projects
- Commute time = productive time

**Learning 3: Prioritize Well**
- Don't sacrifice friends/family for work
- Balance essential
- Take time off (weekends for mental health)

**Learning 4: Winter > Summer**
- Use cold months for focused work
- Summer: Can't compete with good weather
- Leverage seasonal patterns

**Learning 5: Work Holidays/Coworking**
- Combines social + productive
- Keeps mental health good
- Surrounded by motivated people = momentum

## 6. Monetization Model

**Pricing Tiers:**
- Free: Basic features
- Premium: $X/month for advanced

**Revenue Reality:**
- Low seasonal: ~$6K/month (off-season)
- High seasonal: ~30K/month (Christmas/gift-giving times)
- Average: ~$12.5K/month = $150K/year

**Margins:**
- ~99% (server costs main expense)
- Highly profitable at small scale

**Customer Acquisition:**
- Mostly organic (word-of-mouth)
- In-app reviews at right moments (after completing action)
- Zero paid advertising budget

---

## 7. The Secret Sauce - App Store Reviews

**Strategy:**
- Ask for reviews at PERFECT moments
- Example: After user adds wish or completes task
- When user feels good = more likely positive review
- Timing critical (not random)

**The Flywheel:**
- Good reviews → Better App Store ranking
- Better ranking → More organic downloads
- More downloads → More potential reviews
- Compounding effect

**Implementation:**
- Early integration of easy review system
- Asked right moment
- Never annoying (only after success)
- Result: High ratings = organic growth engine

---

## 8. Building While Employed - The Keys

**Key 1: Align with Employer**
- Transparency = trust
- "I'm building Wishlist as side project"
- Most employers okay with non-competing side projects
- Shows initiative

**Key 2: Choose Right Project Type**
- Easy to build in hours (not months)
- Don't pick projects requiring 40+ hours/week
- Work on small, modular improvements
- Finish tasks in 2-4 hour blocks

**Key 3: Focus on Users First**
- Monetization later
- Users = momentum
- Revenue comes naturally

**Key 4: Set Realistic Goals**
- Don't aim for "$1M/year from day 1"
- Aim for "one stranger downloaded my app"
- Early milestone = major victory
- Compound from there

## 11. Advice for Side Project Builders

**Do:**
- Start small (simple problem)
- Build for users you know
- Listen obsessively to users
- Monetize after product-market fit
- Take care of mental health
- Celebrate small wins

**Don't:**
- Chase perfection (ship MVP)
- Forget friends/family for work
- Build what YOU think is cool (build what users want)
- Expect overnight success
- Compare to others
