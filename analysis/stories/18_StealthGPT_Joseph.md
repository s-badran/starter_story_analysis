## 15. Lessons & Insights for First-Time Builders

**Green Flags (What Works):**
- Timing around trending topics (ChatGPT AI detection)
- Free trial with limited use (10 transformations) before paywall
- Multiple pricing tiers for different use cases
- Google Ads as repeatable CAC channel
- Product-market fit = organic discovery (Phil video)
- Marketing > product (can scale with right distribution)

**Red Flags (What Doesn't Work):**
- Core product could be disrupted (detectors improving)
- Heavy ad spend required to maintain growth
- Regulatory risk (schools might ban humanizers)
- Churn unknown (subscription retention is critical)

**Actionable Frameworks:**
1. **Timing:** Look for trending topics (ChatGPT) + build solution quickly
2. **Free Trial:** Allow users to experience product before paying
3. **Pricing:** Multiple tiers for different use cases (students vs. professionals vs. institutions)
4. **Paid Media:** Test multiple channels; find winners and scale aggressively
5. **Team:** Don't let lack of "pedigree" prevent hiring; find people via social media

**Advice to Aspiring Builders:**
- "Marketing is just important, if not more important than the product"
- "If you can't get distribution, you're toast"
- "Never been a better time to build" - AI tools, cheap infrastructure, global audience
- "Build and launch, don't overthink"
- "Have a laptop and wifi; that's all you need"
