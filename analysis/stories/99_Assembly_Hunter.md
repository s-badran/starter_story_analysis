## 7. Key Operational Decisions & Frameworks
**Decision 1: Product-Audience Fit**
- Match creator expertise to business type
- Example: Ali Abdaal taught YouTube → YouTube agency
- Sam Parr known for short-form → short-form agency
- Misalignment = failed business (not done)

**Decision 2: Productized Scope**
- Define exactly what customer gets
- "6 YouTube videos/month" not "YouTube services"
- Clear expectations prevent scope creep
- Premium pricing justified by specific deliverables

**Decision 3: Creator as Partner, Not Celebrity**
- Creators equity stakes in companies (not affiliate)
- Instrumental in business direction and decisions
- Share revenue/growth with creators
- Better model than temporary celebrity endorsement

**Decision 4: Scale Fast, Then Optimize**
- Move fast, intentionally break things
- Hire fast (even wrong hires teach lessons)
- Organize after scaling
- Better to scale first, organize second

**Decision 5: Build for Cash Flow**
- All businesses pursue cash flow from day one
- No VC-style growth-at-all-costs pressure
- Productized services = immediate revenue
- Margins matter as much as growth

**Decision 6: Fragmented Market Selection**
- Choose markets where multiple winners possible
- Not winner-take-all (too much competition)
- Room for differentiation (creator positioning)
