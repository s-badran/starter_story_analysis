# Story Index: Colin - SheetsResume ($20K/Month in 2 Months, AI Resume Builder)

**Founder:** Colin (Resume expert, recruiter background, founder of Sheets & Giggles)
**Company:** SheetsResume.com (AI resume builder)
**Achievement:** $20K/month in second month, ~100% margins
**Model:** Lifetime access AI tool
**Pricing:** $99 lifetime, $29 weekly
**Background:** 6+ years resume expertise, millions helped on Reddit

---

## 1. Colin's Background - Resume Authority

**Career Path:**
- Started at Bridgewater Associates (world's largest hedge fund)
- Fired after 5 months
- Pivoted to recruiting
- Worked recruiting for company that hired him
- Adored recruiting, natural talent

**The Reddit Opportunity:**
- February 2018: Posted resume feedback on Reddit
- Got 500+ upvotes
- DMs flooded: "Can you look at my resume?"
- Light bulb: "No one knows how to make good resume"

**Building Authority:**
- Wrote long resume guide (do's and don'ts)
- Post went viral on Google (still #1 result)
- Top post on r/jobs for years
- 6 years: Answered 20,000+ questions
- Free help: 20,000 people

**Scaling Services:**
- Eventually partnered with Nate (recruiting partner)
- Started resume review service: Couple hundred bucks
- Raised pricing: $300, $400 increasingly
- Demand insane, but hesitated raising further
- Reason: "People going through hard time, don't have cash"

**The AI Insight:**
- Nate (software engineer at Eventbrite): "Why not build AI version?"
- Build plan: 4 months development
- Colin: UX notes + resume rules for AI training
- Launched: August 26 (current year)
- First week: $1,000/day

---

## 2. SheetsResume - The Product

**What It Does:**
- AI analyzes resumes
- Provides feedback and suggestions
- Explains improvements
- Can regenerate with AI help
- Guide: How to improve formatting, content, keywords

**Core Value:**
- Expert resume feedback at scale
- Previously: $100-400 manual review
- Now: Automated, instant
- 100% margin (software vs services)

**Pricing Strategy:**
- $99 lifetime access (main offer)
- $29 weekly option (for job search urgency)
- Deliberately: No monthly subscriptions
- Reason: "Don't predatory pricing during hard time"

**Why Lifetime:**
- Resume used once (during job search)
- Monthly recurring = wrong model
- Lifetime = fair pricing + perceived value

## 8. The Framework for Success

**Framework 1: Find Your Expertise**
- "What do you do better than anyone?"
- "Help people with this thing for free"
- Prove: Solving real problem
- Eventually monetize

**Framework 2: Validate Before Building**
- "Validate idea by doing it not for sale or non-scalable"
- Did manual reviews first
- Proved demand existed
- Then built product

**Framework 3: Build Business Identity**
- Brand identity map (Techstars free template)
- Understand: Voice, value props, demographics
- 2-page business plan
- Document before building

**Framework 4: Execute, Don't AI for Everything**
- Many founders tempted to AI everything
- Colin: "Don't use AI for brand/strategy"
- "Use human judgment for vision"
- Use AI for execution (building product)

**Framework 5: Build Real Team**
- Can't force co-founders
- Can only attract through:
  - Track record
  - Kindness
  - Opportunity clarity
  - Existing relationship

## 11. The Lessons

**Lesson 1: Authority First, Monetize Second**
- 6 years helping for free
- 20,000 people helped
- Then monetized with confidence
- Authority = moat

**Lesson 2: AI Enables Scaling Services to Software**
- Can't scale services profitably
- AI bridges: Automate what was manual
- Manual: $100-400, 3 hours
- AI: $99 lifetime, instant

**Lesson 3: Pricing Reflects Values**
- Could charge monthly subscription
- Chose not to (predatory)
- Lifetime = fair + perceived value
- Customers appreciated values alignment

**Lesson 4: Build for Your Customer**
- Colin: "Target specific customer, not everyone"
- Job hunters = clear segment
- Simple value prop = attracts right people

**Lesson 5: Solopreneur + AI + Small Team = Scalable**
- Colin: 5-10 hours/week
- Nate + one partner handle rest
- Minimal team, massive scale
- AI = labor replacement

## 12. The Advice

**Start with Expertise:**
- "What do you do better than anyone?"
- Help people with that thing
- Build trust through helpfulness
- Then monetize

**Build Business Foundation:**
- Brand identity map (not AI)
- Business plan (2 pages, human judgment)
- Then build product
- Don't rush to building

**Validate Without Building:**
- Do it as service first
- Prove demand exists
- Then automate with software/AI
- Higher conviction, lower risk

**Start Business, Don't Get Stuck in Job:**
- "Best case scenario: Keep current job and succeed"
- "Worst case: Already in job, not more stuck"
- "No lose scenario"
- Action beats perfect timing

## Summary

Colin demonstrates:

1. **Authority + Monetization**
   - 6 years building authority (free)
   - Monetized with confidence
   - Audience trusted = immediate sales

2. **AI Transforms Service to Software**
   - Manual review → AI-powered tool
   - Scales from 1-to-1 to 1-to-many
   - Margins transform (services vs software)

3. **Simple Model Scales**
   - $99 lifetime vs complex pricing
   - Clear value prop
   - Easy decision for customer

4. **Small Team + AI + Focus = Results**
   - 3 people part-time
   - AI handles labor
   - $20K/month in month 2

5. **Values-Based Business**
   - Refused predatory pricing
   - Customers appreciated
   - Builds loyalty

6. **Timing Matters**
   - AI moment (OpenAI cheap credits)
   - Resume market proven
   - Window of opportunity

**For Service Providers:** Colin's model (authority → validation as service → automation via AI → software monetization) provides roadmap for service-to-software transition. Build authority first, validate demand with service, then automate.
