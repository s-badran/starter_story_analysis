# Story Index: Aaron Spivak - Hush Blankets (Weighted Bedding)

**Founder:** Aaron Spivak (co-founder: Lior)
**Company:** Hush Blankets (Weighted Blankets/Bedding)
**Revenue Status:** $48M+ (sold for $48M in 4 years)
**Category:** E-Commerce / Physical Products
**Business Model:** Direct-to-consumer + marketplace
**Stage:** Acquired/Exited

---

## 1. Founder & Discovery

Aaron Spivak started as typical entrepreneur (lemonade stands, juice shops). Met Lior who worked in online business. Together they saw 300,000 monthly searches for weighted blankets. Started during full-time jobs (Aaron: juice shop 6am-2pm, Lior: software company).

**The Problem:** Weighted blankets existed but were expensive, low quality, and not specifically targeted to active lifestyles or diverse needs.

---

## 2. MVP & Launch

**Validation:** Used basic images + one sample to create pre-order site. Ran Google ads on "weighted blankets" keyword (most expensive). First sale made $20. Next day: 3 sales. Extrapolated: "We're going to buy Ferraris!"

**Beginner's Luck:** January launch: 1 → 3 → 5 → 20 sales per day. Thought they'd found the secret.

---

## 3. Crisis & Customer Discovery

**The Crash:** July in Toronto (30°C hottest month) = 0 sales most days. Seasonal product problem. Sitting on unsellable inventory. Lior called: "Let's shut it down." Aaron's moment of truth.

**Breakthrough:** Called every customer with Calendly link. Asked: Why'd you stop buying?

**Customer Insight:** "We love the blankets. It's amazing. But I'm sweating in places I've never sweat before. It's disgusting. I'm putting it in my closet and in the winter, I'll pull it back out."

**Aha Moment:** Create cooling weighted blanket! Problem solved year-round demand.

---

## 4. Product Innovation

**The Fabric:** Created proprietary "Iced Fabric" (coolest in world, keeps you cold without water/fans). Cost: $100,000 for first roll. They had $4,000.

**Solution:** Kicked ass Kickstarter (set $25K goal, raised $1M+ in 30 days). Proved they had 3,000 people on phone calls saying they'd buy.

---

## 5. Scaling & Product Line

**Rapid Product Expansion:**
- Hush Ice Blanket 1.0 (viral on Dragon's Den, 14-season first pitch to increase valuation on live TV)
- Hush Ice 2.0
- Hush Ice Pillows (3,000 sold in 72 hours)
- Hush Ice Bedsheets
- 2020: $20M annual sales

**Dragon's Den:**First 14-season pitch where valuation increased during debate on live TV. Went viral on Netflix.

---

## 6. Revenue & Exit

**Timeline:**
- Year 1-2: Bootstrap to $20M revenue
- Year 3: $40M goal, planning exit
- Year 4: Acquired by largest sleep company in Canada
- Total: $0 → $48M in 4 years

**Key Success Factor:** Community building through storytelling. Pop-up store opening day: 1,500 customers (vs. competitor Alo with 700). "That's why we storytelled."

---

## 7. Key Lessons

**Customer Obsession:** Called every customer personally to understand why they stopped buying. That single conversation revealed the billion-dollar product idea (cooling feature).

**Storytelling:** "Storytelling is the most important thing when marketing a brand. Storytelling is the emotions and the magic that people want to feel."

**Community Over Product:** People cared more about Aaron and Lior as founders than the product itself. Vulnerability about bootstrap journey resonated.

**Persistence:** When faced with shutdown ultimatum (bank account ~$0), found creative solution (Kickstarter) instead of giving up.

---

## Summary

Aaron and Lior took $4,000 to $48M exit in 4 years by solving real problems (seasonal weighted blanket issue through cooling feature discovery) and building intense community through storytelling and vulnerability about their bootstrap journey. Acquired by Canada's largest sleep company.

# Story Index: Thomas Lee - FE International (Business Acquisitions)

**Founder:** Thomas Lee
**Company:** FE International (M&A Platform for Online Businesses)
**Revenue Status:** $100M+ in business volume facilitated
**Category:** B2B / Marketplace / Business Services
**Business Model:** Commission-based M&A brokerage
**Stage:** Scaled marketplace, potentially profitable

---

## 1. Founder Journey

Thomas started in college (2008-2009) by flipping domain names, then websites. First deal: $100 → $500. Self-funded using credit card. By 2010, founded FE International after college.

**Philosophy:** Started with nothing. Built skills through repetition. Moved from $100 deals to $100K deals to $1M+ deals. "The process is what matters, not the deal size."

---

## 2. Business Model Evolution

**Phase 1: Course/Book Sales** - Wrote book on buying/selling websites. Made $30,000 to fund full-time business.

**Phase 2: Accidental M&A** - Course/book buyers asked: "Can you sell my business?" Thomas realized he could facilitate sales without putting up capital. Built business around this.

**Phase 3: Scaled Platform** - Hired team, standardized process. Applied lessons from small deals to larger deals ($1M → $20M → $100M).

---

## 3. Key Success Factors

**Finding Deals:** Email outreach to founders. Personal, tailored messages (not generic). Built relationships, not transactions.

**Valuation:** Multiple of profit (not revenue). Small business: 4-10x annual profit. Good businesses: Higher. Bad businesses: Lower or not sellable.

**Commission Structure:** Tiered: 15% on $100K deals down to single digits on $100M+ deals.

**Profitability Metrics:** Growing, evergreen businesses with recurring customers perform best.

---

## 4. Key Lessons

**Specificity:** Detailed, personalized outreach beats mass email.

**Process > Deal Size:** Same process works whether buying for $100 or $100M.

**Data-Driven:** Use metrics (profit margins, growth rate) to make decisions.

**Hire Early:** Delegation critical for scaling beyond founder.

**Customer Confidence:** Don't assume people won't sell to you. Small relationships lead to bigger deals.

---

## 5. Valuation Philosophy

**For Sellers:**
1. Know your profitability (not revenue)
2. Show growth (buyers pay more for growing businesses)
3. Prove recurring revenue model
4. Build systems (buyer wants to buy a business, not a job)

**For Buyers:**
1. Start small (learn process)
2. Buy something undermanaged (improve it)
3. Leverage skills you have (buy businesses in your wheelhouse)
4. Don't worry about size initially (learn on small deals)

---

## Summary

Thomas built $1B+ marketplace for buying/selling online businesses by starting small ($100 deals), learning the process deeply, and systematizing that process to scale to larger transactions. Key insight: The M&A process is learnable. Specificity in outreach matters. Data-driven decision making beats gut feel.

# Story Index: Jure Kravcar - Four Businesses ($60K/MRR)

**Founder:** Jure Kravcar
**Company:** Rummer (Car Device), Parakeet AI, Optibase (A/B Testing), Apply Agent
**Revenue Status:** $60,000 MRR across 4 businesses
**Category:** SaaS / Physical Products / AI Tools
**Business Model:** Multiple subscription + software models
**Stage:** Growing portfolio approach

---

## 1. The Founder & Portfolio

**Background:** Developer (age 26) inspired by social network (The Social Network) and tech entrepreneurs as kid. Started building apps at 13-14 (never made money). Worked at Microsoft for 1.5 years before starting full-time.

**Key Insight:** Entrepreneurs get 10x results for 10x effort. Employees get same salary regardless of output. This drove decision to go independent.

---

## 2. Business Portfolio

**Business 1: Rummer** ($18K/MRR estimated)
- Device plugs into car OBD2 port
- Generates fake engine sounds
- Launched 2 years ago, 6,000 units sold, $300K revenue
- 200M+ organic TikTok views
- Product-led growth (viral content)

**Business 2: Parakeet AI** ($35K/MRR)
- Real-time AI interview assistant
- Launched 6 months ago
- Credit-based pricing (not subscription)
- Differentiator: "No subscription" positioning

**Business 3: Optibase** ($6.5K/MRR)
- A/B testing tool for Webflow
- B2B SaaS, launched 1 year ago
- Organic growth (Webflow app store, Google, word of mouth)

**Business 4: Parakeet Apply Agent** ($1K+/MRR)
- AI agent applies to jobs for users
- Launched 2 weeks ago
- Already $1K in sales

---

## 3. Go-to-Market Lessons

**Key Principle:** "Bet on organic short-form content" - Most profitable marketing for indie hackers. Just need phone, can get millions of views.

**Rummer Success:** 200M organic views → 6,000 orders → $300K revenue. Proof that product-led short-form content works.

**Channel Strategy:** Pick ONE channel for 2 months. Test rigorously. Double down on what works. Diversify later.

**Idea Validation:** Look at competition. Can you build better product OR market better? If yes, space is yours.

---

## 4. Key Lessons

**Context Switching:** Managing 4 businesses = lots of context switching. Morning: Technical product issue. Afternoon: Which TikTok hook to use.

**Monetization:** Fit monetization to product. Parakeet AI: Credit-based (one-time use) not subscription (recurring). Freemium works for network effects (Rummer).

**MVP Timeline:** "If you can build V1 in 2 weeks vs. 6 months, you have 2x higher chance of success." Speed > perfection.

**Hiring Content Creators:** Sent hundreds of emails to TikTok creators. Urban (co-founder of Rummer) believed in it. Revenue split: Incentives aligned.

---

## 5. Technical Stack

- Next.js + React (modern web framework)
- Speechmatics (transcription)
- ChatGPT (AI)
- Cursor (AI coding)

---

## Summary

Jure built 4 different businesses totaling $60K/MRR by focusing on: (1) Product-market fit in niches with existing demand, (2) Organic short-form content for distribution, (3) Founder collaborators (content creators) with revenue splits, (4) Quick iteration and MVP-first mentality.

# Story Index: Steph France - Gold Mining Framework

**Founder:** Steph France
**Company:** Steph France Consulting / Framework Creator
**Framework:** Gold Mining Framework (AI-powered idea generation to landing page)
**Stage:** Framework/Content Creator, growing audience

---

## 1. The Framework

Steph created "Gold Mining Framework" - 6 tools, 5 prompts, 45 minutes from nothing to potential million-dollar idea + complete landing page without writing code or copy.

---

## 2. Six Steps

**Step 1: Find Market** - Use market idea expander prompt with Claude to expand niche (health → stress management → guided meditation).

**Step 2: Validate Demand** - Use Google + Keyword Everywhere + Google Trends to confirm search volume + trending direction.

**Step 3: Gather Data** - Mine Reddit threads where people discuss problems in that market. Use special Google query to find relevant threads.

**Step 4: Process Data** - Use AI prompts to extract pain points, generate market gaps, identify business ideas.

**Step 5: Select Idea** - Choose which business idea resonates with you from the generated options.

**Step 6: Create Landing Page** - Use landing page prompt creator to generate Lovable prompt that generates complete landing page automatically.

---

## 3. Results

Created co-parenting app landing page (Transition Garden) in 1 hour without any prior knowledge of co-parenting market. Landing page included:
- Hero section
- Feature list (child-friendly countdown tool, conflict-minimizing handoff protocols, etc.)
- FAQ
- Email capture form

All AI-generated, all market-researched, ready to validate.

---

## 4. Key Lessons

**Three Core Markets:** Health, Wealth, Relationships - people always spend money here.

**Customer Language:** Use exact wording from Reddit threads to speak customer's language in copy.

**AI + Human:** AI tools handle data processing, human provides strategy + validation.

**Markdown Formatting:** Using markdown with LLMs provides better structure and results.

---

## Summary

Steph created a replicable framework for finding million-dollar ideas using AI tools + market research. Demonstrates that idea generation isn't magic - it's systematic process of observing market demand, understanding customer pain, and crafting solution messaging.

# Story Index: Pontus & Victor - Cursor Directory

**Founder:** Pontus (with co-founder Victor)
**Company:** Cursor Directory (Directory of Cursor AI Rules)
**Revenue Status:** $34,000-$35,000 MRR
**Category:** SaaS / Directory / Community
**Business Model:** Freemium directory + premium features + ads
**Stage:** Highly profitable solopreneur project

---

## 1. The Idea

On flight to France, Pontus discovered Cursor (AI code editor). Realized there was no centralized place to find Cursor custom rules. People had to find obscure GitHub gists or forums.

**The Insight:** People needed a directory to discover and share Cursor rules in one place.

---

## 2. MVP in 3 Hours

- Figured out needed in minutes
- Victor: Designed in Figma
- Pontus: Started Next.js project, hardcoded JSON with rules found online
- 30 minutes: Something clickable on Vercel
- 3 hours total: Complete MVP with domain

---

## 3. Launch & Growth

- Posted on X (Twitter)
- 1M+ impressions on posts
- Hacker News front page
- YouTubers covered it
- Steady growth as community grew around Cursor
- 2.2M unique visitors since launch
- 49,000 registered users

---

## 4. Business Model

**Free tier:** Browse rules
**Monetization:**
- Job listings (companies pay to post)
- Featured MCP (Model Context Protocol) showcase
- Generate your own cursor rules (tool)
- Eventually: Premium features

**Financial Reality:**
- $35,000 MRR
- Operating cost: ~$500/month
- Maintenance: 3 hours/month
- **Profit margin: 99.8%**
- Fully open source (anyone can fork)

---

## 5. Design as Competitive Advantage

"Design is more important than ever... All of them usually looks the same. So it's not much that you need to be different on, but you need to find the tonality and the angle."

Had design system from previous startups (Mide). Enabled fast, beautiful execution.

---

## 6. Why Directories Still Work

People think directories are dead. They're not. They work when:
- Niche is small enough to be curated
- Problem is discovery/finding things
- Community validates and ranks options
- Timing catches wave (Cursor adoption curve)

---

## 7. Key Lessons

**Start it:** "When you start building and when you commit to something, you eventually getting dialed in, you create your own luck."

**Tech Stack:** Choose tools you know. No new learning curve. Speed > optimization.

**Ship fast:** Don't overthink. Shipping beats perfect.

**Design matters:** In AI-generated world, design is actual differentiation.

---

## Summary

Pontus and Victor built a $34K MRR directory in a weekend (literally 3-hour coding sprint) by spotting a market gap (no central place for Cursor rules), building MVP fast, and leveraging community + good design. Now 99.8% profit margins with 3 hours/month maintenance. Proves directories aren't dead if solving real discovery problem for active community.
