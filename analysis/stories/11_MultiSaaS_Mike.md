# Story Index: Multiple SaaS Apps Portfolio - Mike

**Video ID:** 67zh8_yiPh4
**Founder:** Mike (Australian Developer, ex-Flash Developer)
**Company:** Portfolio of 5 SaaS Apps (Curator IO, Frill Co, Juno Co, Fluke Co, Smile Co)
**Status:** $200K+ MRR across all apps, ~3 years old businesses
**Location:** Australia
**Key Achievement:** Built repeatable framework for launching successful SaaS products

---

## 1. Problem & Validation

**Problem Identified:** Mike had built a flash development agency that he sold, then pivoted to advertising (which he realized wasn't his strength). He discovered his passion was building products.

**Validation Method:**
- Validated market demand by finding ideas that already exist and are proven to work
- Never builds new ideas; focuses on proven markets with existing demand
- Pre-launch validation through private lifetime deals (LTD) generating $30K+ per app

**Key Insight:** "I like to build ideas that can't fail" - Mike's core philosophy centers on minimizing risk through market validation before building.

---

## 2. Industry & Market Opportunities

- **Social Media Aggregation** - Curator IO serves events/websites
- **Customer Feedback Tools** - Frill Co solves feedback & product roadmap visibility
- **Digital Signage** - Juno Co targets cafes, gyms, schools, shops
- **No-Code Product Tours** - Fluke Co simplifies onboarding without developer involvement
- **B2B Group E-cards** - Smile Co (upcoming) for business communication

**Market Selection Pattern:** Mike picks markets where competitors exist but there's room for better UX/pricing

---

## 3. Product & MVP Strategy

**Product Development Philosophy:**
- Prioritizes design quality heavily (good design sells)
- Tech-heavy founding team: front-end dev, back-end dev, designer, general operator
- Always starts with co-founders (4 founders, 25% equity each)
- Starts with ~$10K MRR to cover costs
- After $10K MRR, splits profits among founders (founders-first model)

**MVP Approach:**
- Identifies competitors' features that customers want most
- Builds minimum viable feature set
- Gets product to market quickly for user feedback
- Every team member focuses on UX/product quality

**Key Metric:** All 5 apps launched using same repeatable playbook with high success rate (no failed apps)

---

## 4. Go-to-Market & Customer Acquisition

**10-Step Playbook (Mike's Core System):**

1. **Pick proven ideas** - Never build new ideas; replicate existing successful markets
2. **Decide MVP scope** - Research competitor features customers want
3. **Offer lifetime deals** - $59-$100 one-time payment for early adoption
4. **Never give free accounts** - Always charge; paying users provide more feedback
5. **Sell to LTD communities** - Target private groups, Reddit, Facebook, X
6. **Start content writing** - Blog posts, landing pages (early SEO)
7. **Launch on AppSumo** - Marketplace + select offering for reach
8. **Final private LTD** - Last chance before price increase
9. **Get Trustpilot/G2 reviews** - LTD customers become ambassadors
10. **Reddit/Quora** - Answer competitor questions authentically

**Channels Used:**
- Private Ltd communities (30K+ raised from single Frill Co LTD)
- Twitter/X for organic reach
- AppSumo marketplace
- Review aggregator sites
- Content marketing (SEO-first)

**First Customer:** Varies by app; typically from LTD or early adopter communities

---

## 5. Pricing Strategy

- **Lifetime Deal Model** - $59-$100 one-time payment (entry point)
- **Subscription Model** - Premium recurring pricing after LTD phase
- **AppSumo Pricing** - Lower margin option on marketplace
- **Strategy:** Use LTD to raise capital (~$100K per app from LTD phase), then transition to recurring

**Key Insight:** LTD community is highly motivated and willing to help/promote if product succeeds

---

## 6. Scaling & Growth

**Growth Philosophy:** Bootstrap and stay lean; focus on profitability, not growth at any cost

- Minimal reinvestment in advertising
- Keep staff minimal and distributed
- Focus on building for founders (lifestyle businesses, not exits)
- Target: $1M MRR across portfolio within 5 years, smallest team possible

**Scaling Mechanics:**
- Strong product-market fit from day one enables organic growth
- Design quality drives word-of-mouth
- SEO from early content continues to compound
- Marketplace (AppSumo) provides growth acceleration

## 15. Lessons & Insights for First-Time Builders

**Green Flags (What Works):**
- Proven markets with existing demand
- Small, ideologically-aligned founding teams
- Design-first approach
- Bootstrapped, founder-focused philosophy
- Lifetime deals as capital raise + validation

**Red Flags (What Doesn't Work):**
- Chasing "sexy" unvalidated ideas
- Platform dependencies (API reliance)
- Team misalignment or founder fallout risk
- Overextending with hires too early
- Overspending on marketing

**Actionable Frameworks:**
1. **Market Selection:** Find 3 successful competitors already earning good revenue
2. **Team Hiring:** 4 co-founders, 25% each; must align on lifestyle vs. exit
3. **Launch Sequence:** LTD → AppSumo → Content/SEO → Recurring subscription
4. **Profitability Check:** App should hit $10K MRR before team takes significant salaries

**Mike's Philosophy:** "Don't just create something customers want. Create something you love building." - The intersection of market validation and founder passion is where sustainable businesses are built.

---

## Analysis Summary

Mike represents a systems-first founder who has de-risked the startup process through rigorous methodology. His 10-step playbook isn't revolutionary, but it's disciplined and proven. The key insight is his market selection philosophy: **never build new ideas, always replicate proven markets with better execution.** This dramatically reduces failure risk and enables him to focus energy on execution rather than validation.

His approach is ideal for founders who want lifestyle businesses with high profitability and minimal team complexity, but may not appeal to those seeking venture-scale growth or outsized returns.
