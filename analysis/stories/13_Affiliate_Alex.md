## 15. Lessons & Insights for First-Time Builders

**Green Flags (What Works):**
- Affiliate marketing has zero barrier to entry (no product, no funding needed)
- SEO has long payoff but is stable once established
- Outsourcing content enables scaling
- Brand partnerships provide recurring revenue
- Building while employed = reduced pressure, more strategic decisions

**Red Flags (What Doesn't Work):**
- Affiliate marketing is slow (6-12 months to first revenue)
- SEO is unpredictable (algorithm changes can crater rankings)
- Reliance on partners = revenue dependency (brands can change commission structures)
- Requires patience and faith through long valleys

**Actionable Frameworks:**
1. **Niche Selection:** Look for categories that are new/growing but not yet saturated
2. **Content Strategy:** Write the first comprehensive comparison for emerging products
3. **Keyword Research:** Target long-tail keywords (lower volume but easier to rank for)
4. **Freshness:** Continuously update existing content rather than just writing new
5. **Scaling:** Once one site works, replicate model across new niches (portfolio approach)

**Advice to Young Alex:**
- "Get started. Don't engineer the outcome. Pick a broad audience, find their problems, help them."
- "Understand you'll have to pivot a bunch"
- "Where you start is not where you end"
