## 4. The Flipping Playbook - 7-Step System

**Step 1: Spot Trends in App Store**
- Go to App Store, see #1 in each category
- Check top 20: similar apps?
- Use Sensor Tower: what's their revenue? (>$100K MRR = trend exists)
- Verdict: If multiple apps >$100K MRR, there's momentum

**Step 2: Add Your Edge**
- Lots' edge: Faith/Christian market
- Question: Where can you uniquely win?
- Strategy: Be first in that micro-niche with a mainstream product

**Step 3: Make Single-Player**
- Mistake: Social features that require 2+ people
- Solution: Design for solo use initially
- Add social as secondary feature later
- Why: Easier marketing to 1 person not 2

**Step 4: High Shareability OR High Retention**
- **Shareability**: Users share content/assets (not app itself)
  - Example: Magic Music - users share songs with friends
  - Friend sees "Create your own" button → downloads app
- **High Retention**: Users keep coming back
  - Example: PrayScreen - blocks apps unless they pray (forces daily use)

**Step 5: Simple Tech Stack & Operations**
- Use no-code/low-code when possible
- Keep infrastructure simple
- Goal: Buyer can run it without technical know-how
- Clean operations = easier to sell

**Step 6: Grow to 10-20K MRR with Specific Channels**
- **UGC First**: Create rage-bait content (controversial, shareable)
- **Meta Ads**: Launch on Meta with proven UGC videos
- **CPI Target**: 30-50 cents cost per install (very efficient)
- **Timeline**: 6-12 months to reach 10-20K MRR

**Step 7: Sell strategically**
- **Timing**: Last 3 months should show uptick (not declining)
- **Platforms**: Microacquire, Twitter for listings
- **Valuation**: 2-4x EBITDA (profit) typical for consumer apps
- **Speed > Price**: Sell to 3rd highest bidder if they close in 3-4 days
  - They feel lucky, close fast, deal doesn't fall through
  - Higher price = longer negotiation = higher chance of failure
