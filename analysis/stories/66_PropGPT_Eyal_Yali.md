# Story Index: Eyal & Yali - PropGPT ($30K/month Sports Analytics App)

**Founders:** Eyal & Yali (Israeli co-founders)
**Company:** PropGPT (sports betting analytics app)
**Revenue:** $30K/month ($360K+ ARR)
**Model:** Freemium mobile app + machine learning predictions
**Background:** Tech entrepreneurs with distribution experience
**Strategy:** Distribution first, then rebuild product for actual users

---

## 1. The Key Insight - Distribution Without Product

**The Unusual Story:**
- Had distribution figured out from Day 1
- Revenue was still stuck at $1-2K/month
- Problem: Product wasn't good enough

**The Realization:**
- Distribution doesn't matter if product sucks
- "If your product is shit, nobody's going to buy it"
- They had proven marketing channels but still failed

---

## 2. The Product - PropGPT

**What It Does:**
- Sports betting analytics platform
- Machine learning algorithm predicts best picks
- Users get access to models + analyze own picks
- 40,000+ downloads, 3,000+ paying customers

**Why It's Valuable:**
- Solves specific problem: Which bets are profitable?
- Target audience: Sports bettors (proven spending power)

---

## 3. The Initial Launch - Good Distribution, Bad Product

**The Build:**
- Took 5 months to build first version
- ChatGPT was new (used it, but still took time)
- Launched mid-NFL season (good timing)

**The Distribution:**
- Eyal had worked with other successful app founders
- Leveraged influencer marketing from day 1
- Started spending "few thousand dollars" on influencers
- Results: 20 downloads/day, 5-10 converted to trial

**The Problem:**
- 45% conversion: Downloads → free trial (good signal)
- 13% conversion: Trial → paid (terrible signal)
- Result: Stuck at $1-2K MRR despite great marketing

**The Interpretation:**
"What does that really say about your users?"
- Users THINK it's great (45% try it)
- But when they USE it: Not what they hoped for (13% stay)
- Product-market fit was wrong

---

## 4. The Pivot - Four Months Rebuilding in Silence

**The Decision:**
- "Shut down all marketing, spent four months completely rebuilding from scratch"

**What Changed:**
- Users wanted different value prop than original design
- Original: Self-analysis tool (complex, requires thinking)
- New: Just give me the right answers (simple, actionable)

**The Result:**
- April 15: Launched new version at $1,700 MRR, 15 downloads/day
- May: Conversion to paid > 50% (from 13%)
- June: Growth skyrocketed
- July: Hit $40K MRR, 2,000 downloads/day

---

## 5. The Product Excellence Framework

**Step 1: Understand Your Customer**
- Know who you're building for
- Know their pain points specifically

**Step 2: Listen to Data**
- Became obsessed with numbers
- 45% conversion → trial (great)
- 13% conversion → paid (terrible)
- Gap tells story

**Step 3: Obsess Over Analytics**
- Track WHERE users drop off
- Identify which features drive engagement
- Know what's compelling about your app
- Communicate that in marketing

**Tools Used:**
- PostHog (analytics)
- Superwall (paywall optimization)
- RevenueCAT (subscription management)

**Example:**
- If onboarding is killing conversion: Know it
- If users aren't clicking features: Know it
- Use data to identify problems

---

## 6. Monetization - Getting Conversions Right

**Initial Problem:**
- Paywall poorly positioned
- User experience didn't match expectations

**Superwall Optimization:**
- Tested different paywall designs
- A/B testing proved critical
- Eventually hit 50%+ conversion (vs 13%)

**The Key:**
- It wasn't paid marketing that changed
- It was product + messaging alignment

---

## 7. Growth Channels - Influencer Marketing

**Why Influencer Marketing Works:**
- Followers deeply engage with influencers
- High conversion when influencer says "I love this"
- Easier to convert than cold ads
- One viral video = trajectory change

**Real Example:**
- 70th video: 600K views
- ARR jumped from $8K to $38K in 3 days
- Single piece of social content = 5x business

**Why This Matters:**
- Organic social can still be planned/optimized
- Finding right format = replicable growth

## 9. The Unit Economics

**Monthly Costs (at $30K MRR):**
- Superwall: $0.20 per conversion (cost)
- RevenueCAT: ~1% of revenue
- Data APIs: ~$100/month (real-time sports data)
- Neon database: ~$10/month
- LLM costs: ~$20/month
- Marketing: ~$10,000/month
- **Total: ~$10,150/month**

**Margins:**
- Revenue: $30K/month
- Costs: $10K/month
- **Profit margin: ~50%**

**Key Driver:**
- Marketing is biggest cost (UGC creators)
- Everything else relatively cheap

---

## 10. Profitability Insight

**Why Product Quality Matters:**
- Good product: 50% margins, sustainable growth
- Bad product: High CAC, low retention, death spiral

**Eyal & Yali proved:**
- Fixing product > scaling bad product
- 4 months of rebuild = 15x growth (from $1.7K to $30K+ MRR)

---

## 11. Co-founder Advice

**From Eyal:**
"Have a co-founder who you will be able to lean on and that will be able to lean on you when things get hard."

**From Yali:**
"Be honest with yourself and scientific about whether what you're working on has enough demand."

**The Framework:**
1. Prove sufficient demand exists
2. If yes → worth 100x time investment
3. If no → pivot or kill

---

## Summary

Eyal & Yali demonstrate:

1. **Distribution without good product = failure**
   - Had marketing figured out (influencers)
   - Product still sucked
   - Stuck at $2K MRR despite ads working

2. **Product quality > marketing spend**
   - 4 months of rebuild > $10K/month marketing
   - Good product makes marketing efficient

3. **Data obsession finds problems**
   - 45% → 13% drop-off told entire story
   - Fixed product experience
   - Conversion went from 13% → 50%

4. **One viral moment can 5x business**
   - Single TikTok: 600K views
   - ARR: $8K → $38K in 3 days

5. **Co-founder is essential**
   - During 4-month rebuild: needed support
   - Both founders matter

**For aspiring founders:** The key insight: You can have everything figured out (distribution, marketing, budget) but if product doesn't deliver on promise, you're stuck. Eyal & Yali show that recognizing this and fixing it = breakthrough growth.
