---

## 1. Founder Background & Journey
**Founder:** Erikas Paskauskas
**Origin:** Lithuania (currently Paris-based)
**Starting Point:** Designer making $2/hour, later freelancing
**Current Status:** $4.5M ARR from 5 Shopify apps with 90% profit margins

## 2. Problem Discovery & Opportunity Identification
**Discovery Process:** While freelancing with Shopify experience, Erikas noticed:
1. Indie developers/solo hackers built apps with poor UX (technical, complex)
2. VC-backed/corporate apps had great UX but moved slow (months/year for features)
3. Gap between two extremes: great UX + fast execution

**The Opportunity:** Market gap for apps with excellent user experience, fast iteration, built by lean team (vs. solo devs or bloated corps)

**Validation:** Discovered Shopify Partners page showing "top 25% of developers making $300K/year"
- Realization: Far more lucrative than freelancing
- Had Shopify experience as designer → perfect positioning to build apps with strong UX

## 3. Product/Service Details & Monetization
**Company:** Kaching (Name for portfolio of 5 apps all solving Shopify problems)

**App Portfolio:**
1. **Kaching Bundles:** Product bundling for AOV increase (400K MRR)
2. **Kaching Post Purchase Upsell:** Post-purchase offer functionality
3. **Kaching Pop Up Upsell:** In-app upsell prompts

5. (5th app not detailed but part of portfolio)

**Monetization Model:** Subscription pricing with tiered plans based on usage

## 4. Go-to-Market Strategy & Customer Acquisition
**GTM Approach:** Community + Validation-first strategy

**Strategy Details:**

**1. Pre-Launch Community Building:**
- Joined Dropshipper Discord communities
- Active in e-commerce Facebook groups
- Posted screenshots of NOT-YET-PUBLISHED app design
- Post went viral: 100+ likes, comments asking "where to download"
- First validation: people thought app was live because they wanted it

**2. Free Launch Strategy:**
- Launch app for free to reduce friction and maximize installs
- Grandfather clause: all users who downloaded during free period get lifetime access
- Monetize only new users after building large free base
- Strategy: maximize reviews, app store rankings, organic visibility first

**3. Review Optimization & Reputation:**
- 95% of reviews come from customer support (not in-app prompts)
- Top-notch support team (technical, can dig into code)
- 24/7 customer support
- Gamified support team bonus system: track reviews per agent, monthly leaderboard, raffles
- "Persuader of the Month" award: based on review conversion rate

**4. Organic App Store Growth:**
- Free launch → high install volume → high review volume → app store ranking boost
- Ranking boost → more organic discovery → more installs → viral loop

## 7. Key Operational Decisions & Frameworks
**Decision 1: Become Proven Marketplace Winner (Not Indie Hacker)**
- Analyzed competitors: solo indie devs (poor UX), VC-backed corps (slow)
- Positioned Kaching between two: excellent UX + fast execution
- Designer background provided competitive advantage in UX
- Execution speed required lean team + async processes

**Decision 2: Free Launch → Monetize Later**
- Launch free to maximize installs, reviews, app store ranking
- Grandfather free users (loyalty + testimonials)
- Monetize only new users once product established
- Maximizes organic growth before monetization friction

**Decision 3: Support-Driven Reviews**
- 95% of reviews come from customer support interactions
- Gamified support team incentives
- Technical support team can debug customer issues (add value, increase satisfaction)
- Reviews are lifeblood of app store visibility

**Decision 4: Idea Selection Framework**
- Easy to build (months, not years)
- Broad market (works for any Shopify store)
- Low competition (avoid crowded spaces)
- Result: 5 apps all in "AOV increase" category solving merchant pain points

**Decision 5: Remote Async Operations**
- No office, no US employees
- Hire globally for specialized roles
- Communication via Slack, Linear (task management), Intercom (support)
- Culture of async work enables lean profitability

## 9. Lessons Learned & Insights
**Lesson 1: Platform Specificity Beats General SaaS**
- Building on Shopify ecosystem easier than standalone SaaS
- No customer acquisition needed (Shopify app store does marketing)
- Pre-built customer base hungry for solutions
- Massive advantage over traditional SaaS with zero audience

**Lesson 2: Designer UX Is Competitive Advantage**
- Indie hackers often build technically competent but ugly products
- Founder's design background created clear differentiation
- UX quality drives reviews, satisfaction, word-of-mouth

**Lesson 3: Support Quality Drives Growth**
- 95% of reviews come from support, not product
- Exceptional support justifies higher pricing
- Gamified incentives align support team motivation with business goals

**Lesson 4: Free Launch Maximizes Visibility**
- Counterintuitive: free can be better for growth than paid
- Maximizes app store ranking, reviews, word-of-mouth
- Monetization happens naturally once product established

**Lesson 5: Remote Async Team = Profitability**
- 90% margins possible because team is distributed and async
- No overhead of office, management, or expensive talent
- Requires discipline in async communication and process

**Lesson 6: Niche Selection Matters**
- AOV increase is a clearly defined problem
- Broad market (any Shopify store wants higher AOV)
- Enough room for 5 apps solving different approaches (bundles, upsells, etc.)
