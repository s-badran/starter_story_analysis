## 2. Business 1: Rummer (Car Device)

**The Product:** Device that plugs into car OBD2 port, generates engine sounds based on real RPM data.

**Development:**
- Built while working on Shopify stores
- Discovered car diagnostics device at car show
- Realized: Can read live RPMs and generate audio
- Light bulb: Generate engine sounds + sell as product

**Validation:**
- Product inherently viral (funny, controversial)
- Can tell compelling story
- 10-second TikTok hook potential

**Go-to-Market:**
- Founded co-founder Urban (content creator) through hundreds of emails to TikTok creators
- Urban believed in it, second video went viral
- Revenue split model (incentives aligned)

**Results:**
- Launched 2 years ago
- 6,000 units sold
- $300K revenue lifetime
- 200M+ organic TikTok views (no paid ads)
- Steady income

## 3. Business 2: Parakeet AI (Interview Assistant)

**The Product:** Real-time AI assistant for job interviews. Transcribes, provides answers via ChatGPT in real-time.

**Development:**
- Proof of concept in web app (real-time transcription + ChatGPT)
- Two weeks to MVP to sale
- Rapidly iterated based on feedback

**Tech Stack:**
- Next.js + React
- Speechmatics (transcription, ~$1/hour)
- ChatGPT API (~$2-3K/month)

**Monetization Innovation:**
"Credit-based is such an obvious way to monetize this product. And that's a big selling point for us because a lot of our competition does subscription."

- No subscription model
- Pay-per-use credits
- Counter-positioning against competitors

**Results:**
- Launched 6 months ago
- $35K MRR (fastest growing)
- Validated demand for alternative to subscription

## 6. Marketing Philosophy - "Bet on Short-Form Content"

**Core Principle:**
"If you're trying to start an online business right now, the way to do it is to bet on organic short form content. Most other marketing channels are saturated to a point where you just coming in is going to be so hard for you to break through and make something that's profitable."

**Why Short-Form Works:**
- Free to create (just phone)
- Algorithmic amplification (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts)
- Can get million views with no budget
- "Only constrained by your willingness to work hard and make the right content"

**Rummer Example:**
- 200M organic views
- Converted to 6,000 orders
- ~$300K revenue
- Zero paid advertising

## 7. Product Development Framework

**MVP Philosophy:**
"If I can build a product from zero to making the first sale in two weeks, but it takes you six months, it's going to be very hard for you to compete with me."

**Speed Focus:**
- MVP in 2 weeks for Parakeet AI
- Proof of concept in web app
- Real-time transcription + ChatGPT answers
- Minimal features to validate demand

**Competitive Advantage:**
Building 2x faster = 2x higher success rate.

---

## 8. Content Creation & Marketing Strategy

**Batching Approach with Co-founder Urban:**
- Creates batches of content
- Generates 5-10 different hooks
- 3 hooks × 5 main segments × 3 endings = many combinations
- Tests combinations to find winners
- Replicates successful format

**Content Variables Tested:**
- Different people on camera
- Different hooks (top 10, best way to, I hate when...)
- Different accounts (sometimes accounts with 48 followers beat main account)
- Different editing (fast-paced vs. slow)
- Different lighting (casual vs. cinematic)

---

## 9. Key Lessons

**Context Switching Challenge:**
"Morning I might be improving the product and working on some super niche technical issue. And then by the end of the day, I'm deciding which stupid TikTok hook we're going to use that's going to get us the most views."

The skill of context-switching rapidly between technical and marketing is a superpower.

**Monetization Matters:**
"The way you monetize needs to fit the way your product is being used."

Parakeet AI: Credit-based (people use once or twice) vs. subscription (implies ongoing usage).

**Product Quality > Marketing:**
"If you have the type of product that we have with Rummer, you need to find that consistency and repetition in the marketing itself."

But with Parakeet AI: "The most important thing is you have a good product. There's no amount of marketing... that's going to be a good product and a service people are happy with."

## 10. Idea Finding & Validation

**Discovery Method:**
"You will not come up with ideas randomly sitting on your sofa. You need to be out there doing stuff."

Rummer came from:
- Building Shopify stores (observing e-commerce)
- Buying used car (car context)
- Discovering diagnostics device (technical discovery)
- Connecting the dots (engine sounds + OBD2 data)

**Validation:**
1. Check if someone already solved it better
2. Identify if there's room to build better product OR market better
3. Test with niche community first
4. Leverage short-form content for distribution

## Summary

Jure Kravcar built a $60K+ MRR portfolio across 4 different businesses (hardware, AI SaaS, B2B SaaS, automation) by:

1. **Building what you observe** - Ideas come from real problems observed
2. **Moving fast** - 2-week MVP beats 6-month perfection
3. **Betting on short-form organic** - No paid ads needed with good content
4. **Finding co-founders with complementary skills** - Urban for content/marketing
5. **Monetizing appropriately** - Credit-based for one-time use, subscription for recurring
6. **Context-switching rapidly** - Technical work + marketing work same day

Most importantly: Jure proves that a young, solo developer can build multiple profitable businesses by focusing on real market needs, moving fast, and leveraging organic short-form distribution channels that don't require capital.
