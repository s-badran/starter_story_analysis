## 1. Problem & Validation

**Problem:** Earning credit card points and airline miles is manual (spreadsheets, research). No tool automates stacking coupons, promo codes, rewards.

**Personal Pain Point:** Founder was expert at earning rewards; realized market gap

**Validation Approach:**
- Tried generic channels first (Product Hunt, HN, Indie Hackers) - failed (95-96% bounce)
- Tried influencer outreach (300-400 emails) - got 1 response
- Realized needed targeted, problem-specific channels
- Switched to Reddit and Facebook groups (not indie hacker communities)
- Engagement in communities for 3+ months before top-level post

## 4. Go-to-Market

**5-Step Playbook (Avnish's Framework):**

**Step 1: Brainstorm 5-15 keywords** related to target users
- Credit cards, rewards, travel hacking, etc.

**Step 2: Find communities** (Reddit + Facebook groups)
- Used "Map of Reddit" tool to find related subreddits
- Searched Facebook groups by interest

**Step 3: Observe behavior first**
- What are people talking about?
- How are they talking about it?
- What language/etiquette do they use?

**Step 4: Set goals for each community**
- Goal = get feedback vs. get users vs. understand problems
- This shapes how you post

**Step 5: Two-phase posting approach:**
- Phase 1: Comment-only (low risk, test messaging)
- Phase 2: Top-level posts (after building credibility)

**Bonus: Keyword alerts** (F5bot for Reddit/email alerts on keywords)
- Get real-time when people mention your topic
- Go engage immediately

**Final: Be helpful organically**
- Share tips naturally (earned right to mention product)
- Build trust → can then promote

**Specific Example (Rakuten group):**
- Created spreadsheet of Rakuten + AmEx offers
- Posted link in Facebook group
- 1,500+ people visited website from single post

## 10. Challenges & Lessons

**Key Lesson:** Understanding target customer is MORE important for marketing than for product

**Biggest Realization:**
- Spent months finding right customer segment
- Wrong channels (Product Hunt, HN, IA) had wrong audience
- Right channels (Reddit, Facebook groups) had exact customers

**Advice to Young Self:**
- Never forget about target customer
- Use customer understanding for BOTH product AND marketing
- Know where your customers hang out online
