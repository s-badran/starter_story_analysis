## 1. Problem & Validation

**Problem:** Fitness apps provide bad workouts (Fitbot had "weird and dangerous" workouts). AI-generated workouts lack proper customization.

**Validation:**
- Used existing app (Fitbot), found problem through personal use
- Built better version (Fitbot UI + proper workout engine)
- Posted MVP on Reddit with technical breakdown
- Got 300K impressions, couple thousand users from single post
- Immediate feedback confirmed product-market fit

**Key Insight:** Finding better implementation of existing solution (not inventing new category)

---

## 2. Industry & Market

**Industry:** Mobile fitness / Consumer app

**Market:** Huge (fitness is always monetized, app space growing)

**Customer:** Gym-goers, strength trainers, intermediate-advanced fitness enthusiasts

**Beachhead:** Developers + gym-goers (found Reddit audience with both attributes)

---

## 3. Product & MVP

**MVP Timeline:** 2-3 months initial

**Features:**
- AI-powered custom workouts
- Personalization (gender, weight, age, experience, goals, training frequency)
- Video demos for exercises
- Integration with Apple Health, Strava
- Recovery tracking
- 24/7 support chat with humans (not AI)
- Hard paywall before first workout (converted high percentage)

**Tech Stack:**
- Frontend: React Native, Expo
- Backend: .NET for core functionality
- Internal tools: Next.js, React
- Development approach: Used Cursor (AI-assisted)
- Thoughtful design choices (paywall positioning, human support)

## 10. Challenges & Lessons

**Key Learnings:**
1. **Validate before spending on ads** (product-market fit AND willingness to pay)
2. **Localize early** (South America cheaper, Spanish-speaking founders advantage)
3. **Start small with creators** ($50 for content, use CapCut/AI video tools)
4. **UGC is king** (user-generated content outperforms professional ads)
5. **Copy what works** (study competitor ads, replicate, improve)
6. **Know when to pivot vs. persist** (dragged previous startup too long)

**Previous Mistake:** Ran influencer marketing business that wasn't sustainable
