**Company:** AI Kreia (AI home renovation tool)

## 3. Idea Validation Framework

**How Pauline Finds Ideas:**

"I usually find ideas when I encounter some problem, either me or friends. When it's friends, like for a career, I ask questions, a lot of questions. Really, you have this problem, but when, how frequent you have this problem. I really think, am I able to do better? Am I able to build something around it? Am I able to monetize it and to find clients?"

**The Five Questions:**
1. Is there a real problem?
2. How frequent is it?
3. Can I build better solution?
4. Can I build a business around it?
5. Can I monetize it + find customers?

**Decision Gate:** Only start if "yes" to all five.

## 4. The MVP Process

**Philosophy:**
"My opinion, the sooner you ship, the better. Because sometimes you imagine your clients want this, but what they actually want is this."

**AI Kreia MVP:**
- Simple feature (one key capability)
- Bad quality at first
- Built in ~1 month
- Then: 1 year of improvement work

**The Reality Check:**
"If you don't have clients when you start, don't expect everything to be perfect."

## 5. Customer Validation

**How She Validated:**

1. **Build MVP** (1 month)
2. **Show to real users** (real estate agents from network)
3. **Gauge response** ("that's awesome, I want to buy it now")
4. **Get paid** (actual payment = validation)

**Key:** Real money from real customers = proof of concept, not just opinions.

## 7. Part-Time vs. Full-Time Business Building

**Pauline's Observation:**
"I think it's really different when you are working on a day job or you are hacking full time."

**Day Job Tradeoffs:**
- Pro: Financial security
- Pro: Can take risks (validated before quitting)
- Con: Limited time (nights/weekends)
- Con: Mental energy divided

**Full-Time Advantages:**
- Faster iteration cycles
- More time for customer development
- Can take bigger strategic swings
- No safety net (more focus)

**Her Strategy:**
- Stayed at IBM until sustainable income
- Reduced risk of failure catastrophe
- Now full-time with proven model

## 10. Key Lessons

**Lesson 1: Don't Quit Too Early**
"I wasn't ready to make a big jump of becoming an indie hacker because I needed money as well."

Most side projects fail. Keep job until proven.

**Lesson 2: Ship Imperfect**
MVP was "bad quality" but functionally solved the problem. Perfect is enemy of done.

**Lesson 3: Talk to Customers**
Asking real estate agents "when do you have this problem" prevented building for imaginary market.

**Lesson 4: Get Paid Fast**
Getting customers to pay quickly (not just feedback) = real validation.

## Summary

Pauline built a €8K/month home design AI tool by:

1. **Finding real problem** - Homeowners want instant renovation visualization
2. **Part-time validation** - Kept income while building/testing
3. **MVP speed** - 1 month build, then continuous improvement
4. **Customer first** - Got real people to pay before scaling
5. **Hybrid monetization** - Both one-time and recurring revenue
6. **Full-time commitment** - Quit IBM only after proven sustainability

Her story proves: The best time to go full-time is when your side project is already paying your bills. Then you can accelerate growth without financial risk.
