## 15. Lessons & Insights for First-Time Builders

**Green Flags (What Works):**
- Early entry into emerging platforms = outsized advantage
- Gaming as service (recurring revenue) vs. single transaction
- Brand partnerships = recurring, high-margin revenue
- Influencer/creator connections critical for growth
- Youth audience creates viral potential

**Red Flags (What Doesn't Work):**
- Platform risk is real (Roblox could change revenue share)
- Dependence on algorithm/platform rules
- Requires native experience (Cole started age 8-9)
- Highly competitive once platform matures

**Actionable Frameworks:**
1. **Platform Selection:** Pick emerging platforms where adults haven't dominated yet
2. **Early Entry:** Start before mainstream attention
3. **Creator Partnerships:** Early movers have advantage in influencer relationships
4. **Diversified Revenue:** Games + brands + content = multiple revenue streams
5. **Build Community:** Engagement = algorithmic advantage + brand loyalty
