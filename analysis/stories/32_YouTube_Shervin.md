## 15. Lessons & Insights for First-Time Builders

**Green Flags (What Works):**
- Be consistent (2x/week for years)
- Focus on search-based content (evergreen)
- Build affiliate partnerships (higher margins)
- Niche down (tech + gadgets)
- Surround with other creators
- Post when categories launch (timing)
- Authentic reviews (not fake enthusiasm)

**Red Flags (What Doesn't Work):**
- Virality-focused (unsustainable)
- Daily posting burnout
- AdSense-only monetization (low margins)
- Perfect, over-produced content
- Isolation from other creators

**Actionable Frameworks:**
1. **Content Mix:** 80% evergreen search, 20% trending
2. **Monetization:** Affiliate (80%) + AdSense (20%)
3. **Posting:** 2x/week is optimal sweet spot
4. **Community:** Surround with other creators
5. **Patience:** 2-3 years before 1,000 subscribers
6. **Network:** Ask bigger creators for feedback

**Advice to Young Shervin:**
- "Don't quit your job until you have audience momentum"
- "Work 1 day/week on content before full-time switch"
- "Consistency beats virality"
- "Affiliate > AdSense"
- "Build with other creators (not in isolation)"
