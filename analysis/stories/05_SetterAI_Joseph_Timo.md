## 1. Problem & Validation

**Problem:** Sales reps spend time on follow-ups and qualification. AI can automate calling and qualifying leads.

**Validation Strategy (The Deposit Framework):**
- Created fake landing page with fake demo (video from 11labs)
- Used warm domain (existing domain with Google indexing)
- Set H1 keyword: "cold call automation"
- Used Ahrefs to find low-competition keyword
- Got immediate Google indexing (Google Search Console request)
- Zero marketing spend to validate

**First Customer:** Got $500 Stripe payment BEFORE building product
- Discovered billion-dollar company needed this
- Knew market signal was strong

**Key Insight:** "Validation is when buyers swipe credit card" - tangible proof > surveys
