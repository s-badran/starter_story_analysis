# Story Index: Jack Fricks - Multi-App Strategy ($10K/month from Basement)

**Founder:** Jack Fricks (Self-taught developer)
**Companies:** Curiosity Quench + PostBridge
**Revenue:** $10K/month combined ($3K + $7K)
**Model:** Mobile app + SaaS web app
**Background:** McDonald's worker → college dropout → "mom's basement hacker"
**Strategy:** Solve own problems, 1 hour daily marketing, organic growth only

---

## 1. The Background - From McDonald's to Independence

**The Journey:**
- Worked at McDonald's, saved money
- Dropped out of college (realized didn't want job)
- Tried multiple side businesses: book, t-shirts, crypto YouTube
- Eventually: Learning to code

**The Motivation:**
"I would often tell my friends and myself that I would rather be homeless than work a job."

**The Truth:**
- Built multiple things with ChatGPT
- Put errors into ChatGPT until working product
- Didn't have skyrocket growth immediately
- Had to keep iterating for months

---

## 2. The Two Apps

**App 1: Curiosity Quench ($3K MRR)**
- Problem: Scrolling phone all day, no good apps
- Solution: Habit/hobby action plans to replace scrolling
- Business model: $30/year subscription + 7-day free trial
- Downloads: 100,000+
- Revenue: $3K/month, $60K+ lifetime

**App 2: PostBridge ($7K MRR)**
- Problem: Jack spending 30 min/day copying posts to all platforms
- Solution: Schedule content to Instagram, TikTok, Twitter at once
- Business model: $9/month starting price + tiers
- Revenue: $7K/month, $40K in first 5 months
- Key advantage: Jack priced it at what he'd pay (competitors 10x more expensive)

---

## 3. The Idea Validation Framework

**Jack's Process:**
1. Is this a problem I would pay for?
2. If yes → it's painful enough to build
3. If no → don't build it

**Validation on Platform:**
- Post 60-second video on TikTok
- Point-and-shoot, talk to camera
- 15 people commented: "I need this"
- Kept making videos
- Same results
- Verdict: "I should probably make this"

---

## 4. The One-Hour-Per-Day Marketing Strategy

**The Framework:**
Spend exactly 1 hour daily marketing.

**What That Looks Like:**

**Setup (2 days, 15 min each):**
- Create TikTok and Instagram accounts
- Scroll to warm algorithm (don't want bot flags)
- Save viral videos in niche
- Comment and follow people in space
- (Don't post yet - let it settle)

**After warmup, daily process:**
1. Open CapCut (learned in 1 hour)
2. Use proven templates from viral videos
3. Add Jack's app/call-to-action (3 seconds max)
4. Post same day (don't schedule far out - iterate faster)
5. Track what works

**The Content Strategy:**
- Find winning format
- Milk it till dry
- Rinse and repeat

**The Format Jack Found:**
- Two-by-two image template
- 6 seconds long
- Funny caption
- Pinned comment: "Go download our app"
- Intentional mistakes (astrology vs astronomy = engagement wars in comments)

**Results:**
- 300+ templates posted
- 60-70K signups from TikTok
- Hundreds of millions of views
- All organic (no paid ads)

---

## 5. The Posting Philosophy

**Daily vs Batching:**
- Jack posts every single day (same day creation)
- Reason: Iterate faster
- Batching: Can't test next week's trends

**Multiple Account Strategy:**
- 3 Instagram accounts
- Spread posts 3-4 hours apart
- Prevents algorithm flagging as spam
- Uses different videos/metadata (algorithms detect duplication)

## 7. Building in Public on Twitter/X

**Jack's Personal Brand Strategy:**
"The easiest way to grow personal brand is to not grow personal brand at all."

**Just build cool things and share them.**

**Key insight:**
Most people share quotes/aphorisms no one cares about.
If you share what you're actually doing, it's 10x more interesting.

**Breaking Through Fear:**
- Started new social accounts to escape real-life people
- Only way to overcome fear: Just do it
- "No one really cares. They're thinking about themselves."
- When you're authentic, people feel it

**Result:**
- 300+ posts on Twitter about building
- Built genuine audience
- Audience became customers

---

## 8. Monetization Strategy

**Mobile App (Curiosity Quench):**
- Paywall after initial value delivery
- Free trial showing problem/solution
- Gets user excited
- Then: Paywall for premium

**Web App (PostBridge):**
- No free tier
- 7-day free trial (credit card required)
- Can cancel anytime
- Discount on yearly

**The Psychology:**
- Mobile: "Try it, then pay" (impulse more likely)
- Web: "Prove you serious with card, I'll give 7 days free" (committed users only)

**The Leverage:**
- Free trial + yearly discount drove revenue up significantly

## 12. Key Lessons

**Lesson 1: Solve your own problems**
- Both Jack's apps solve his own pains
- That's why they work

**Lesson 2: One hour daily > sporadic bursts**
- Consistency beats intensity
- 1 hour every day > 8 hours weekends

**Lesson 3: Iterate > perfect launch**
- Post every day to iterate faster
- See what works tomorrow, not next month

**Lesson 4: Build in public = audience**
- Authenticity resonates
- "Prove it" with shipped products

**Lesson 5: Persist through early resistance**
- Months 1-4: Nothing
- Months 4-8: Traction
- Months 8+: Revenue
- Average: 3-4 years to $10K/month
