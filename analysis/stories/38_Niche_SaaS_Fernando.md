## 15. Lessons & Insights for First-Time Builders

**Green Flags (What Works):**
- Solve own problem (scratching itch)
- Niche down (specialize, not generalize)
- Product Hunt launch (credibility)
- Free tools (natural funnel)
- Simple > complex
- 4-hour focus > 8-hour scattered
- One feature > feature-rich
- Affordable > cheap positioning

**Red Flags (What Doesn't Work):**
- General products (compete with Canva)
- Complex MVP (perfectionism)
- Multiple features (dilutes focus)
- 8-hour work (burnout)
- Feature creep (kills clarity)

**Actionable Frameworks:**
1. **Idea:** Validated market + specific niche
2. **Building:** 10-day challenge (urgency)
3. **MVP:** Barebones (not buggy-free)
4. **Launch:** Product Hunt
5. **Funnel:** Free tools → Main product
6. **Positioning:** Affordable, not cheap
7. **Expansion:** Start narrow, expand to suite

**Advice to Young Fernando:**
- "Niche down (0.1% of market = good enough)"
- "Simple > features (people want time saved)"
- "4 hours > 8 hours (focus > hours)"
- "Lifestyle > growth (freedom > scaling)"
- "Pair features (carousel + other tools)"
