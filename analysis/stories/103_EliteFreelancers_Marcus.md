# Story 103: Marcus - Niche B2B Marketplace ($200K+/ARR)
## B2B SaaS Marketplace, Freelancer Vetted Marketplace, 2-Year Journey

---

## 1. Founder Background & Journey
**Founder:** Marcus
**Starting Point:** Frustrated with finding quality freelancers in specific niche
**Previous Experience:** 10 years in enterprise software
**Key Event:** Built personal network of 500+ vetted freelancers, realized business opportunity
**Current Status:** $200K+/ARR, 2,000+ projects facilitated
**Key Characteristic:** Enterprise sales background, quality-first mindset, niche domination

---

## 2. Problem Discovery & Opportunity Identification
**Problem:** Enterprise clients can't find high-quality freelancers in specialized niches
- Generic marketplaces (Upwork, Fiverr) flooded with low-quality providers
- Time spent vetting exceeds value of project
- Quality variation massive (from $5/hr to $500/hr for same skillset)
- Need for trusted, vetted supply

**Opportunity:** Verticalized marketplace with strict quality standards
- Solve via curation (not volume)
- Higher prices justified by quality guarantee
- B2B buyers willing to pay premium for reliability

---

## 3. Product/Service Details & Monetization
**Product: EliteFreelancers (Niche B2B Marketplace)**
- Handpicked 500+ vetted freelancers (aerospace engineering specialists)
- Rigorous vetting: portfolio review + skill tests + client references
- Project matching (AI recommends best fit)
- Quality guarantee: Money-back if unsatisfied
- Integration with Slack, Asana, Monday.com

**Monetization:**
- 25% take rate (vs. 20% Upwork, but higher prices justify it)
- $5,000-$50,000 projects (premium buyers)
- Subscription model for enterprise teams (flat $1,000/month)

**Economics:**
- Gross Margin: 25%
- COGS: Customer support, payment processing
- Retention: 85% (strong for marketplace)

---

## 4. Go-to-Market & Customer Acquisition
**GTM: Direct Sales to Enterprise + Word-of-Mouth**

**Channel 1: Direct Outbound to CTOs/VPs**
- Identified 100 target companies needing specialized talent
- Cold LinkedIn + personalized outreach ("We have 50 vetted specialists for you")
- Demo: Show portfolio of vetted freelancers
- Conversion: 20% of conversations → trials

**Channel 2: Referral Network**
- Each successful project generates 2-3 referrals (word-of-mouth)
- Freelancers refer networks to other firms (network effects)
- Built-in advocates: Both supply + demand sides

**Channel 3: Content Marketing**
- Published 50+ industry reports on freelancer quality trends
- SEO for "find [specialty] freelancers"
- Positioned as thought leader in niche

**Customer Profile:**
- Agencies needing specialized contractors
- Enterprises with variable engineering needs
- Smaller teams requiring full-stack expertise

---

## 5. Business Model & Revenue Streams
**Model:** Verticalized B2B Marketplace (high-touch, high-ACV)

**Revenue Breakdown (Year 2):**
- Commission-based: 60% ($120K/ARR)
- Enterprise subscriptions: 30% ($60K/ARR)
- Freelancer premium features: 10% ($20K/ARR)

**Unit Economics:**
- CAC: $500 per customer (direct sales)
- LTV: $2,500+ per customer
- Payback period: 2 months
- High ACV justified by quality

---

## 6. Team, Hiring & Scaling Strategy
**Team:**
- Marcus: Founder + Sales (enterprise relationships)
- COO: Operations/Vetting (freelancer quality)
- Customer Success: Managing accounts
- Developers: 1 part-time (marketplace operations)

**Scaling to $1M+/ARR:**
- Add sales hires (2-3 AEs by Month 18)
- Expand to adjacent niches (legal, accounting, medical)
- Build supply team (recruiting freelancers)

---

## 7. Key Operational Decisions
**Decision 1: Vertical Specialization**
- Start with aerospace engineering (not "all freelancers")
- Quality > volume strategy
- Deep domain expertise of supply team
- Higher pricing justified by specialization

**Decision 2: Vetting as Moat**
- 20-hour vetting process per freelancer
- Portfolio review + skill tests + references
- Money-back guarantee backed by vetting
- Supply-side switching cost

**Decision 3: Enterprise SaaS Pricing Model**
- Flat $1,000/month tier for unlimited projects
- Attracts agencies with high volume
- More predictable than commission-only
- Hybrid model caps downside risk

---

## 8. Competitive Advantages
1. **Vetting moat:** Only 500 freelancers (not 50,000)
2. **Quality guarantee:** Money-back if unsatisfied
3. **Specialization:** Deep expertise in niche
4. **Enterprise relationships:** Direct CTO connections
5. **Network effects:** Both sides grow together

---

## 9. Lessons Learned
**Lesson 1:** "Specialization beats scale in marketplaces."
- Higher prices, better retention, less competition
- Upwork can't compete in niche

**Lesson 2:** "Vetting is your competitive advantage, not technology."
- Tech is commodity (Upwork has better tech)
- Trust is differentiator (our vetting > generic)

**Lesson 3:** "Hybrid revenue models reduce downside."
- Commission alone is risky (depends on volume)
- Subscriptions provide floor
