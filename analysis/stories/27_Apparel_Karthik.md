## 15. Lessons & Insights for First-Time Builders

**Green Flags (What Works):**
- Start with $500 (capital constraints = creativity)
- Target specific niche (Greek → campus → athletes)
- Build licensing model (barriers to competition)
- Direct customer outreach (campus advantage)
- Build quality relationships with suppliers (moat)
- Multiple brands (diversification)
- Leverage marketplace distribution (Etsy, Google Shopping)
- Be first to new market (NIL ruling = opportunity)

**Red Flags (What Doesn't Work):**
- Manufacturing complexity (1+ year to find partners)
- Legal/licensing risk (surprise $40K bill)
- Founder dependency (Karthik's drive kept it going)
- Supply chain fragility (border delays, freight costs)

**Actionable Frameworks:**
1. **Market Entry:** Target specific niche (Greek organizations)
2. **Direct Sales:** Customer outreach + relationship building
3. **Licensing:** Get college/athlete licenses = moat
4. **Supplier Relationships:** Build vendor portal to reduce their admin
5. **Diversification:** Multiple brands targeting different segments
6. **Marketplace:** Use free distribution (Etsy, Google Shopping)
7. **Timing:** Watch for market shifts (NIL ruling = new opportunity)

**Advice to Young Founders:**
- "Start with what you have ($500)"
- "Build in validated markets (don't create new categories)"
- "Relationships matter (licensing + suppliers)"
- "Work hard early (6 days/week for 2.5 years)"
- "Get parents' pride (as motivator)"
- "Build systems that make partners' jobs easier"
