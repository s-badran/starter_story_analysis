# Story Index: John McAvoy - Moigo ($30K/month Bus Tracking App)

**Founder:** John McAvoy (Full-stack developer)
**Company:** Moigo (Real-time bus tracking application)
**Revenue:** $30K/month ($360K+ ARR)
**Users:** 5M+ downloads
**Model:** App Store monetization (ads + premium)
**Background:** Built during COVID pandemic, exploded via App Store optimization
**Strategy:** ASO (App Store Optimization) mastery, niche focus, user acquisition

---

## 1. The Origin Story - COVID Lockdown

**Starting Point:**
- Built during pandemic
- First app on App Store
- Had existing developer skills
- Wanted "income replacement" during lockdown
- Made decision: "Let's try getting income from App Store"

**Market Signal:**
- COVID = more people on phones
- People seeking entertainment
- Unmet needs in app ecosystem

## 2. The Problem Being Solved

**Bus Tracking Reality:**
- European bus/transit system fragmented
- Many regions don't have real-time tracking
- Buses often tracked unreliably or not at all
- Travelers have information asymmetry

**John's Insight:**
- Built for European market first
- Simple value prop: "Know when your bus arrives"
- Solved real problem with no good existing solution

---

## 4. The Growth Method - ASO (App Store Optimization)

**What is ASO:**
- App Store Optimization
- Like SEO but for app stores
- Optimizing for discoverability in App Store algorithm

**John's ASO Strategy:**

**Component 1: Keywords**
- Tested hundreds of keyword combinations
- Keywords in app name, subtitle, description
- "Real-time bus tracker" vs "Find your bus"
- Data-driven testing

**Component 2: Screenshots & Previews**
- App Store preview videos
- Screenshots telling story
- A/B tested 50+ variations
- Tested: Headlines, images, colors, layouts

**Component 3: App Icon Design**
- Critical for tap-through rate
- Tested dozens of icon variations
- Colors, typography, style

**Component 4: Ratings & Reviews**
- Actively managed reviews
- Responded to negative reviews
- Built features based on feedback
- Better ratings = better ranking

**Component 5: Conversion Optimization**
- Entire user flow tested
- Onboarding optimized for conversion
- Friction removal at each step

**Results:**
- ASO dominated by tech companies (typically)
- John's approach: Scientific, systematic testing
- Result: Massive organic traffic from App Store
- 5M+ downloads with minimal paid ads

## 9. The Challenge - Scale & Maintenance

**Technical Challenges at Scale:**
- API integrations with 50+ transit agencies

**Maintenance Burden:**

## 10. Lessons on Market Selection

**Key Insight:**
- Chose niche market (bus tracking)
- Not oversaturated (unlike ride-sharing, maps)
- Real problem with no dominant solution
- Network effects (more users = better data)

**Why This Matters:**
- Entering market with incumbents = harder
- Entering market with underserved niche = better odds
- Bus tracking: Google Maps not focused, Citymapper fragmented
- Opportunity to own

   - No huge marketing budget

   - Result: 5M users, $30K/month

   - Ratings higher
   - Word-of-mouth better

Result: Sustainable $30K/month with minimal user acquisition cost and 85%+ margins. The model: find real problem, solve it beautifully, optimize for discoverability, let word-of-mouth grow it.
