# Story Index: Nico - Neuroframes ($100K/month at team of 5)

**Founder:** Nico
**Company:** Neuroframes (AI music video generator)
**Revenue:** $100K last month (team of 5), was solopreneur first year
**Category:** AI tool / Creative tools
**Background:** Physicist → PhD → Deep tech startup → AI music videos
**Time to First Revenue:** 2022 start, growing since
**Current Status:** Team of 5, recurring monthly revenue of $100K+

---

## 1. The Founder Journey - Non-Traditional Path

**Academic Background:**
- Studied physics
- Finished master's in 2016
- "Fed up with physics already"
- Pursued PhD anyway (2016-2020)

**The Breakthrough:**
- During PhD: Ran first scientific simulations
- Discovered: "I really like programming"
- Combined computer vision + physics knowledge

**Career Transition:**
- 2020: Finished PhD, joined deep tech startup
- Learned "a lot about AI and programming"
- Worked at intersection of computer vision + physics

**The Decision to Leave:**
- 2022: Left deep tech startup
- Started building Neuroframes
- First full stack software project ever
- All solo first year

---

## 2. Why Nico Left Academia

**The Problem with Physics:**
"Deep down I must say I was never ultra passionate about physics. I was good in it and so I kept on doing it, but I always had the feeling that there would be more for me somehow."

**Core Motivation:**
"I wanted to build something that affects people in the real world, maybe that improves somebody's lives. I think deep down in me there is a wish to be seen in the world."

**The Startup Appeal:**
"Every decision I make has direct impact on the startup, which is really great."

Stark contrast: Academia = individual contribution lost in larger system. Startups = every decision matters.

## 3. The Product - Neuroframes

**What It Does:**
AI music video generator. Takes music and creates visual content automatically.

**Problem It Solves:**
Musicians need visual content for every song. Traditionally difficult + expensive to produce.

**Target Users:**
1. **Hobby/indie musicians** - Primary segment
2. **Professional musicians** - Secondary
3. **AI music creators** - New segment (AI tools generate music, need AI video)

**How It Works:**
Uses multiple AI tools + Nico's orchestration to create music videos from audio.

## 9. Advice for Career Changers

**To People Considering Career Change:**

"I think the most common thing holding people back is two things. One is they are scared that things won't work out. And the second might be that they are scared they can't do it, maybe due to technical challenges."

**His Response:**

"There's really no reason to be scared, to be honest, because there's nothing to lose. You will learn so much by switching careers, by building something your own, that you will easily make up for it later on, even if it doesn't work out."

"People should also not be scared of the technical challenges. It's never been easier to build something."

**The Safety Net:**
"The worst case that can happen is that, yeah, I tried to do something new and you can always go back to get your old job."
