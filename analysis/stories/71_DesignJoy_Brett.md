# Story Index: Brett Williams - DesignJoy ($1.8M+ annual, Solopreneur)

**Founder:** Brett Williams (Graphic designer → Productized service owner)
**Company:** DesignJoy (Unlimited monthly graphic design subscription)
**Revenue:** $1.8M+ annually ($150K/month, solopreneur, 6-hour work days)
**Model:** Productized service (monthly recurring subscription)
**Background:** Graphic designer who saw subscription model, replicated it solo
**Strategy:** Demand-based pricing, clear boundaries, niche clientele, passive income products

---

## 1. The Origin - Spotting an Opportunity

**The Inspiration:**
- Noticed existing agency: "Unlimited graphic design" subscription service
- Had physical location, employees, recurring revenue model
- Realized: "I can do this myself without the overhead"

**The Insight:**
- Most agencies: Project-based or hourly billing
- The opportunity: Subscription model (recurring, predictable)
- The twist: Solo operation (eliminate team costs)

---

## 2. The Launch - Speed Over Perfection

**Day 1 Action:**
- Built simple landing page in 12 hours
- Cost: $29
- Name: DesignJoy
- Pricing: $449/month for "unlimited design"

**Result:**
- First paying customer: Within 24 hours
- Key insight: "Speed beats perfection"
- Momentum over overthinking

---

## 3. Key Strategy #1 - Demand-Based Pricing

**The Psychology:**
- Too-good-to-be-true pricing = fast customer acquisition
- $449/month = clearly unsustainable long-term
- BUT: Generates momentum and builds reputation

**The Progression:**
- Year 1: $449/month
- Year 2: $1,000/month
- Year 3: $3,000/month
- Year 4: $5,000/month
- Current: Up to $8,000/month

**The Math:**
- 16 clients × $5,000 = ~$80K/month
- = $1M+ annually

**Why This Works:**
- Willing to "put in reps" at low price
- Build world-class reputation
- As demand increases, raise prices
- Existing clients "grandfather" at old rates (or move to higher)

---

## 4. Key Strategy #2 - Boundaries (The Game Changer)

**The Problem:**
- High demand = burnout risk
- Could hire team, but Brett wants solopreneur model

**The Solution - Two Critical Boundaries:**

**Boundary 1: Async Communication Only**
- Clients submit requests via Trello board
- NO calls, NO meetings
- All requests in writing

**Boundary 2: One Request at a Time**
- Only one active design request per client
- Can't submit new request until current one approved
- Gives Brett breathing room to deliver quality

**The Results:**
- Clients spend more time perfecting requests (fewer total)
- Brett has time to create masterful work (quality increases)
- Saves countless hours of meetings
- Improves both client satisfaction AND Brett's workload

**Cultural Impact:**
- Some clients dislike boundaries = they churn
- Right clients appreciate efficiency = they stay
- Self-selecting for ideal customer

---

## 5. Key Strategy #3 - The $500 vs $5,000 Client

**Two Client Archetypes:**

**$500 Clients (Low-value):**
- Cheap, demanding, needy
- Frequent refund requests
- Time-intensive, low-loyalty
- High churn

**$5,000 Clients (High-value):**
- Wealthy businesses
- Understand ROI
- Willing to pay for quality
- Long-term retention
- Treat service as business expense

**Why $5,000 is Sweet Spot:**
- Business can write off $5K/month easily
- Compare: Hiring designer ($200K/year) or agency ($30-40K per project)
- DesignJoy at $5K/month = obvious choice

**Value Proposition:**
- 48-hour turnaround (vs weeks for agencies)
- One-click ordering (vs complex procurement)
- No long-term hiring commitment
- Cancel anytime (like Netflix)

---

## 6. Key Strategy #4 - High Demand, Low Touch

**Revenue Side:**
- Design is high-impact (conversion rates, first impressions)
- Companies MUST get this right
- Willing to pay premium

**Expense Side:**
- Brett: Solo (no team)
- 6 hours/day (vs typical 8-10)
- Minimal overhead
- Why? Website design = "build once, use long time"

**The Leverage:**
- High demand + low touch = highly profitable
- NOT all services work this way:
  - Bookkeeping: Too time-consuming
  - Video editing: Too labor-intensive
  - Design: Relatively efficient per dollar

---

## 7. Key Strategy #5 - Build Once, Sell Forever

**Product Innovation:**
- Created "Scribbles" (design templates collection)
- Priced: $4.99
- Downloads: 25,000+
- Passive revenue (29% of income)

**Course Creation:**
- Built "Productize Yourself" course
- Teaches other designers his system
- Premium pricing
- Passive income stream

**The Value:**
- Years of experience → knowledge product
- Monetizes expertise without trading hours
- Compounds over time
- Allows reduced client work while maintaining income

## 10. Key Insights - The Playbook

**Insight 1: Demand-Based Pricing Works**
- Counter-intuitive: Charge LESS initially
- Builds reputation
- Raise prices as demand increases
- Grandfathering protects existing clients

**Insight 2: Boundaries Scale Better Than Hiring**
- Clear rules > team management
- Filters for right customers
- Improves quality
- Reduces work for founder

**Insight 3: Client Quality Matters More Than Volume**
- $5K client > 10× $500 clients
- Better alignment, less demanding
- Self-selecting through price

**Insight 4: Service Choice is Critical**
- Must be high-demand, low-touch
- Design works (build once, long lifespan)
- Bookkeeping doesn't (constant attention)

**Insight 5: Passive Income Products Extend Runway**
- Templates + courses = 25-30% of income
- Doesn't require 1-on-1 delivery
- Compounds over time

---

## 11. Applicability

**Who Can Replicate:**
- Designers, developers, strategists
- Anyone with premium skills + clear value
- Service delivery + product thinking
- Solo or small team

**Who Shouldn't:**
- High-touch services (therapy, coaching may need different model)
- Services requiring constant attention
- Low-value markets

---

## Summary

Brett demonstrates the power of:

1. **Spotting & Replicating** - Saw proven model, adapted it solo
2. **Demand-based Pricing** - Low price initially, scale with demand
3. **Operational Boundaries** - Protect time through clear rules
4. **Client Selectivity** - High-price filters for ideal customers
5. **Service Design** - Choose high-demand, low-touch offerings
6. **Passive Income** - Templates & courses extend income without hours
7. **Solopreneur Leverage** - $1.8M with zero employees, 6-hour days

**The Formula:** Productize a premium service → Charge high enough to work with only ideal customers → Implement boundaries that reduce work → Diversify with products → Enjoy lifestyle freedom.

This is sustainable, scalable profitability without the chaos of team management.
