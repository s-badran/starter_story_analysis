## 3. The Product Strategy - Recurring vs One-Time

**Business Model 1: One-Time Purchase**
- App sells for $19.99 (one-time)
- CAC: Paid ads
- If CAC > $19.99: Lost money
- Model: Difficult, need massive volume

**Business Model 2: Subscription**
- Monthly recurring revenue
- CAC > $19.99 (acceptable)
- LTV: 6-12 months × price
- Model: Works better

**Nico's Pivot:**
- Early apps: One-time purchase
- Later apps: Subscription
- Learned: Subscription scales, one-time doesn't

## 6. The Paid Ads Machine - How It Works

**Step 1: Identify Opportunity**
- Spot gap in market
- OR see competitor succeeding
- OR have personal pain

**Step 2: Build MVP**
- Don't wait for perfect
- Ship fast
- Get to paying customers

**Step 3: Paid Ads Launch**
- Budget: Likely $1K-5K to test
- Target: Specific audience
- Measure: CAC vs LTV
- Iterate: Stop losing channels, scale winning

**Step 4: Scale or Pivot**
- If unit economics work: Scale ads
- If not: Pivot product or model
- If still not: Abandon for next app

**Advantage Over Organically Grown:**
- 6 months vs 2 weeks to traction
- Clear feedback (paid tells you truth)
- Scalable growth (not luck based)
- Valueable to acquirers

## 10. The Lessons

**Lesson 1: Paid Ads = Honest Signal**
- Organic: Can be luck (viral tweet, Reddit)
- Paid: ROI = truth
- LTV > CAC = works
- LTV < CAC = doesn't
- Numbers don't lie

**Lesson 2: Subscription > One-Time**
- One-time: Hard to achieve positive CAC
- Subscription: LTV large enough
- Easier unit economics
- More valuable to acquirer

**Lesson 3: Build, Not Perfect**
- MVP in 2-4 weeks beats 6 months perfection
- Market feedback more valuable
- Failure fast saves months
- Only perfect what's working

**Lesson 4: Selling > Building Forever**
- Exited 2 apps (while others keep building)
- Took capital (compound it)
- Not attached to apps
- Business is return on capital

**Lesson 5: Acquirers Value Revenue**
- Bootstrapped: "Buy my code" ($0 credibility)
- With customers: "Buy my revenue stream" (premium valuation)
- Paid acquisition: "You can scale this" (confidence)
- Difference: 10x valuation
