## 1. Problem Identification & Market Validation

**Problem Solved:** AI coding models hallucinate and make incorrect assumptions. Developers need a knowledge base to ground AI coding tools in accurate information.

**Who Has It:** Beginner to semi-intermediate developers using AI coding tools (Cursor, Windsurf)

**Validation Approach:**
- Found pain point from tweeting about AI prompting on Twitter
- Identified gap: AI coding is exploding but hallucination is a major blocker
- Validated on Twitter: One tweet about "ideal cursor workflow" got massive engagement showing people struggling with hallucinations
- Pre-launch: 1,800+ waitlist signups in 2 weeks with just a landing page
- Beta validation: Talked to beta users, confirmed no one was solving this better way
- **Key Insight:** "Content Audience Fit" approach - validate through Twitter interactions first
